# South Korean Bioindustry Survey

> The South Korean Bioindustry Survey is an annual national statistic overseen by South Korea's industry ministry and the Korea Biotechnology Industry Organization covering eight bioindustry sectors. Published on November 26, 2026, the 2026 survey aggregates full-year 2025 results from 1,343 companies, reporting 26.8236 trillion won in production (+14.2%) and 16.9556 trillion won in exports (+21.4%), while bio-sector investment fell 12.7%.

The South Korean Bioindustry Survey (국내 바이오산업 실태조사) is an annual national statistic overseen by South Korea's Ministry of Trade, Industry and Resources and conducted by the Korea Biotechnology Industry Organization (KoreaBIO) to track production, domestic sales, exports, imports, workforce, and investment across domestic biotechnology businesses. The survey classifies establishments into eight sectors under the Korean Agency for Technology and Standards (KATS) bioindustry classification system and publishes prior-year full-year performance under the current survey year's title. Released on November 26, 2026, via the Korea Bio-Industry Information Service (KBIOIS), the 2026 survey covered 1,343 companies and reported 2025 total production of 26.8236 trillion won (+14.2% year-on-year), exports of 16.9556 trillion won (+21.4%), domestic sales of 9.8680 trillion won (+3.7%), and bio-sector investment of 4.7898 trillion won (-12.7%).

**Also known as:** Korean Bioindustry Survey, 국내 바이오산업 실태조사

## Included businesses, eight sectors, and survey year vs. performance year

The South Korean Bioindustry Survey measures domestic companies operating across eight biotechnology sectors defined by the Korean Agency for Technology and Standards (KATS): biopharmaceuticals, bioservices (including contract manufacturing and testing), biofood, biochemicals and bioenergy, biomedical devices, bio-equipment and instruments, bio-environment, and related bio-resource categories.[4][6] Total production is calculated as the sum of domestic sales and exports.[6]

Because the survey collects finalized full-year corporate figures in the following calendar year, the survey title year is one year ahead of the performance year: the 2026 survey released on November 26, 2026, reports full-year 2025 results from 1,343 companies (+7.4% from the prior year).[1][5]

## Headline 2025 performance in the 2026 survey

According to the 2026 survey results published on KBIOIS and reported by Yonhap News Agency and Newsis, South Korea's bioindustry production rose 14.2% (3.3402 trillion won) to 26.8236 trillion won in 2025, up from 23.4834 trillion won in 2024.[1][4][6] Exports reached 16.9556 trillion won (+21.4% YoY), accounting for 63.2% of total production and roughly 90% of the year's production increase.[3][6]

Total supply (production plus imports) rose 16.6% to 31.6831 trillion won.[5] Bio-sector investment declined 12.7% to 4.7898 trillion won as facility investment fell 29.9% to 1.9554 trillion won following a 146.1% surge in 2024, even though R&D spending grew 4.9% to 2.8344 trillion won.[5][6]

## Production and export breakdown by bioindustry sector

Growth in 2025 was led by biopharmaceuticals and bioservices.[4] Biopharmaceutical production reached 10.0008 trillion won (+18.2% YoY), while bioservices production rose 28.8% to 5.8156 trillion won, the fastest growth rate among the eight sectors.[2][4] Biomedical devices (+12.9% to 2.4256 trillion won) and biochemicals and bioenergy (+11.3% to 3.5438 trillion won) also posted double-digit gains, whereas biofood fell 2.7% to 4.6673 trillion won.[4]

In exports, biopharmaceuticals (7.7851 trillion won, 45.9% share) and bioservices (4.4935 trillion won, 26.5% share, +42.9% YoY) together accounted for 72.4% of total bioindustry exports.[3][4] In domestic sales, biochemicals and bioenergy held the largest share at 2.9414 trillion won (29.8%), followed by biopharmaceuticals (2.2157 trillion won, 22.5%) and biofood (2.1909 trillion won, 22.2%).[4]

## Data coverage limits as of November 2026

As of November 27, 2026, full-year 2026 bioindustry production, export, and investment figures have not yet been compiled and will be surveyed in the 2027 edition of the report.[1][4]

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the South Korean Bioindustry Survey?

It is an annual national statistic overseen by South Korea's Ministry of Trade, Industry and Resources and executed by KoreaBIO that measures production, domestic sales, exports, imports, employment, and investment across domestic bioindustry companies. [1][2]

### Which businesses and items are included, and how does the survey year differ from the performance year?

The survey covers domestic companies across eight bioindustry sectors defined by the Korean Agency for Technology and Standards (including biopharmaceuticals, bioservices, biofood, biochemicals and bioenergy, and biomedical devices). Because finalized annual figures are collected the next year, the 2026 survey reports full-year 2025 performance. [4][6]

### What were the key 2025 figures in the 2026 survey?

Across 1,343 companies, 2025 production reached 26.8236 trillion won (+14.2%), exports reached 16.9556 trillion won (+21.4%, or 63.2% of production), domestic sales totaled 9.8680 trillion won (+3.7%), and workforce stood at 68,133 (+1.9%). [1][3]

### Why did bio-sector investment decline in 2025 despite higher production and exports?

Bio-sector investment fell 12.7% to 4.7898 trillion won because facility investment dropped 29.9% to 1.9554 trillion won following a 146.1% base-effect surge in 2024, even though R&D investment increased 4.9% to 2.8344 trillion won. [5][6]
