# PlayStation Plus

> PlayStation Plus is Sony's three-tier gaming subscription (Essential, Extra, Premium). Its December 2026 Monthly Games for all tiers are Tony Hawk's Pro Skater 3+4, Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2, and Amnesia: The Bunker, claimable from 2026-12-01 to 2027-01-04, while November's Monthly Games remain available through 2026-11-30.

PlayStation Plus is a paid subscription service from Sony Interactive Entertainment that provides PlayStation players with online multiplayer access, monthly downloadable games, cloud storage, exclusive discounts, and tiered game libraries across three plans: Essential, Extra, and Premium. ## PlayStation Plus membership tiers and benefits

PlayStation Plus is a paid subscription service from Sony Interactive Entertainment offering three membership tiers: Essential, Extra, and Premium.[1][5] Across all three plans, members receive online multiplayer access, monthly games, exclusive discounts, exclusive game content, 100GB of Cloud Storage, Share Play, and PlayStation Direct shipping at no extra cost.[5]

PlayStation Plus Extra adds access to the Game Catalog, while PlayStation Plus Premium adds the Classics Catalog, Cloud Streaming, and Game Trials.[4][5] Sony notes that the available game library varies over time, by region or country, and by membership plan.[4]

## PlayStation Plus December 2026 Monthly Games

PlayStation Plus announced its December 2026 Monthly Games lineup on November 25, 2026, via PlayStation.Blog.[1] All PlayStation Plus members can claim Tony Hawk's Pro Skater 3+4 (Cross-Gen Edition for PS5 and PS4), Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2, and Amnesia: The Bunker from Tuesday, December 1, 2026, through Monday, January 4, 2027.[1]

Until Monday, November 30, 2026, members can still claim the November 2026 Monthly Games lineup announced on October 28, 2026: Dead Island 2 (PS5, PS4), Tomb Raider IV-VI Remastered, and Monster Train 2.[3]

## PlayStation Plus November 2026 Game Catalog and Classics

PlayStation Plus expanded its Extra and Premium Game Catalog in November 2026 with titles announced on November 10, 2026, including Death Stranding 2: On the Beach, Like a Dragon: Pirate Yakuza in Hawaii, Atomfall, and Dragon Quest III HD-2D Remake.[2] For PlayStation Plus Premium members, Soul Reaver 2 and Freedom Wars Remastered joined the Classics lineup.[2]

Release timing for Game Catalog titles can vary by region; PlayStation LifeStyle reported that Death Stranding 2: On the Beach, Atomfall, and Dragon Quest III HD-2D Remake rolled out globally on November 17, 2026, while Like a Dragon: Pirate Yakuza in Hawaii rolled out on November 24 in the US, UK, and Japan after a November 17 rollout in other regions.[7] Specific country-by-country differences for December's lineup were not detailed in Sony's November 25 blog post.[1]

## PlayStation Plus Black Friday 2026 offers

PlayStation Plus memberships were included in Sony's Black Friday 2026 promotion, which began on November 16, 2026, and runs through November 30, 2026.[4][6] On the official U.S. PlayStation Plus page, Sony advertised limited-time Black Friday discounts for qualifying users on PlayStation Plus Premium, Extra, and Essential plans.[4]

Sony's November 3, 2026 Black Friday announcement stated that exact offers and availability vary by region and participating retailer.[6]
