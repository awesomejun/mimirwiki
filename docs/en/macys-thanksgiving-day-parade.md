# Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade

> The Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade is an annual New York City parade organized by Macy's since 1924 and broadcast on NBC, Telemundo, and Peacock. Due to severe storm and high-wind warnings, Macy's and New York City officials postponed the 100th parade from Thursday, November 26, to Saturday, November 28, 2026, at 8:30 a.m. ET.

The Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade is an annual holiday parade presented by the department store chain Macy's in New York City, first staged by Macy's employees in 1924. Traditionally held on Thanksgiving morning along a Manhattan route from the Upper West Side to Macy's Herald Square, the parade features giant character balloons, floats, and live performances broadcast nationally on NBC and Telemundo and streamed on Peacock. For its 100th march in 2026, severe weather forecasts including high winds and coastal storms prompted New York City officials and Macy's to postpone the parade from Thursday, November 26, to Saturday, November 28, 2026, at 8:30 a.m. ET. Organizers and news reports described the weather move as the first postponement of the parade to another day in its century-long history.

## Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade origin and holiday tradition

The Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade was created by Macy's employees in 1924 as an annual Thanksgiving holiday celebration in New York City.[1] In 2026, the event marks its 100th march, running along its traditional Manhattan route from the Upper West Side to Macy's Herald Square.[1][5]

Public festivities associated with the parade include the Macy's Balloon Inflation Celebration on the Upper West Side, which NYC311 listed for Wednesday, November 25, 2026, with public viewing from 1:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m. entering at 72nd Street and Columbus Avenue.[2]

## Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade 2026 weather postponement

For the 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade in 2026, organizers and New York City officials postponed the march from Thanksgiving Day (Thursday, November 26) to Saturday, November 28, 2026, at 8:30 a.m. ET.[1][6] During a joint security briefing on November 25, 2026, New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani, NYPD Commissioner Jessica S. Tisch, and Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade Executive Producer Will Coss announced the schedule change, citing severe weather expected on Thanksgiving Day, including high-wind warnings and coastal flooding risks across the Tri-State area.[1][6]

Schedule adjustments for severe weather are coordinated jointly by Macy's, the New York City Mayor's Office, the New York City Police Department (NYPD), and partner municipal agencies.[1][6] Secret NYC and Clarín reported that the 2026 move was the first time in the parade's century-long history that the event was postponed to another day due to weather.[5][6]

## Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade television broadcast and viewership

NBCUniversal serves as the primary broadcast partner for the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade, airing live coverage on NBC and Telemundo alongside streaming on Peacock.[3][6] Ahead of the 2026 holiday season, NBCUniversal announced its holiday programming slate on November 18, 2026, pairing the 100th parade broadcast with the special *America's Parade: 100 Years of Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade* and the 25th anniversary of *The National Dog Show Presented by Purina*.[3]

According to NBCUniversal, the 99th parade broadcast in 2025 set an all-time viewership record for the network.[4]

## Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade 2026 operational status

Following the November 25, 2026 postponement announcement, Macy's Studios Vice President Will Coss stated that organizers were implementing operational adjustments to stage the centennial parade on Saturday, November 28, 2026.[6] As of November 27, 2026, the rescheduled Saturday march has not yet taken place, and final on-site attendance and television viewership figures for the 100th parade remain unconfirmed.[1][6]
