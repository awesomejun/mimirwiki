# TSMC Arizona

> TSMC Arizona is TSMC's semiconductor manufacturing complex in Phoenix, Arizona, spanning an expanded 2,000-acre footprint backed by $265 billion in announced investment covering 10 planned fabs, two advanced packaging facilities, and an R&D center. Fab 1 began 4nm mass production in Q4 2024, Fab 2 targets 3nm production in Q3 2027, and a new groundbreaking is scheduled for December 4, 2026.

TSMC Arizona is the U.S. semiconductor manufacturing subsidiary and multi-fab complex of Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC) located in Phoenix, Arizona. Supported initially by up to $6.6 billion in direct federal funding under the U.S. CHIPS and Science Act for its first three fabs, TSMC expanded its pledged Arizona investment to $265 billion on July 16, 2026, encompassing 10 planned wafer fabs, two advanced packaging facilities, and a research and development center. Its first Phoenix fab entered mass production of 4-nanometer chips in the fourth quarter of 2024, while its second fab has completed main construction and is undergoing tool installation ahead of planned 3-nanometer volume production in the third quarter of 2027. In late November 2026, the White House and TSMC announced that President Donald Trump is scheduled to attend a groundbreaking ceremony in Phoenix on December 4, 2026.

**Also known as:** TSMC Phoenix, TSMC Arizona Corporation

## TSMC Arizona overview and CHIPS Act foundation

TSMC Arizona is Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company's advanced semiconductor manufacturing hub in Phoenix, Arizona (ZIP 85083).[1] Under the U.S. CHIPS and Science Act, the U.S. Department of Commerce awarded TSMC Arizona up to $6.6 billion in direct funding to support an initial investment exceeding $65 billion across three greenfield leading-edge wafer fabs in Phoenix.[1] According to the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), that initial three-fab cluster was projected to create approximately 6,000 direct manufacturing jobs, more than 20,000 unique construction jobs, and tens of thousands of indirect jobs.[1]

## TSMC Arizona July 2026 $100 billion expansion plan

On July 16, 2026, TSMC Chairman and CEO C.C. Wei announced an additional $100 billion investment in Arizona to build at least four more 2-nanometer or more advanced wafer fabs alongside advanced packaging capacity.[2][6] According to the Arizona Commerce Authority, the July 2026 announcement raised TSMC's total pledged investment in Arizona to $265 billion, covering 10 wafer fabs, two advanced packaging facilities, and one research and development center.[2]

TrendForce reported that following TSMC's acquisition of land south of Arizona State Route 303 in 2026, the company plans to integrate its northern and southern Phoenix campuses into a combined 2,000-acre footprint.[3] Tom's Hardware noted that TSMC did not attach a fixed completion schedule to the July 2026 expansion, stating that the pace of construction would be guided by market demand.[6]

## TSMC Arizona operational status versus planned facilities

TSMC Arizona combines active wafer production with multi-phase construction and planning across its Phoenix complex.[2][3]

| Facility / Phase | Process Node / Function | Reported Status (as of Nov 2026) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fab 1 | 4nm chips | In mass production since Q4 2024; producing chips for U.S. customers including Nvidia (Blackwell), AMD, and Apple |
| Fab 2 | 3nm chips | Main construction complete; undergoing equipment move-in and tool installation; mass production targeted for Q3 2027 |
| Fab 3 and subsequent fabs (up to 10 total) | 2nm and more advanced nodes | Construction timelines and southern-site development planned; pace tied to market demand |
| Advanced packaging and R&D | 2 advanced packaging facilities and 1 R&D center | Planned under the $265 billion Arizona roadmap |

As reported by TrendForce citing Commercial Times and Bloomberg, Fab 1 entered 4nm volume production in Q4 2024 and manufactures chips for U.S. customers including Nvidia, AMD, and Apple, while Fab 2 has completed main construction and is installing equipment ahead of targeted 3nm mass production in Q3 2027.[3]

## TSMC Arizona December 2026 groundbreaking ceremony

In late November 2026, the Associated Press reported, citing both the White House and TSMC, that President Donald Trump is scheduled to travel to Arizona on December 4, 2026, to speak at the groundbreaking ceremony for a new TSMC foundry expansion in Phoenix.[4][5] TrendForce reported that the ceremony is expected to mark the start of development at TSMC's newly acquired southern site and be attended by TSMC executives, suppliers, and customers.[3]

As of November 26, 2026, whether the December 4 groundbreaking takes place as scheduled and the final mass-production timelines for the newly announced fabs remain unconfirmed.[3][4][6]
