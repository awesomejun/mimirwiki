# HUAWEI Mate X8

> The HUAWEI Mate X8 is a book-style foldable smartphone unveiled by Huawei in China on November 24, 2026, featuring an 8.2-inch inner OLED display, a 6.54-inch cover screen, a Kirin 9050 Pro processor, HarmonyOS 7.0, and a 6,200 mAh battery. China launch prices start at CNY 12,999, with general sales beginning December 4, 2026.

The HUAWEI Mate X8 is a book-style inward-folding smartphone unveiled by Huawei in China on November 24, 2026. ## HUAWEI Mate X8 announcement and China lineup

The HUAWEI Mate X8 was officially unveiled by Huawei at a launch event in China on November 24, 2026, at 14:30 CST, with pre-sales opening the same day at 18:08 CST.[2][7] According to Huawei's official China purchase guide, the series consists of the standard HUAWEI Mate X8 and the HUAWEI Mate X8 Collector's Edition, both shipping with HarmonyOS 7.0.[2] At the launch event, Richard Yu, chairman of Huawei's Consumer Business Group, stated that Huawei had sold more than 12 million foldable phones worldwide since entering the category with the original Mate X in 2019.[7]

## HUAWEI Mate X8 dimensions and magnetic hinge

The HUAWEI Mate X8 measures 156.9 mm in length, 77.3 mm in width when folded, and 152 mm in width when unfolded, according to Huawei's official specification sheet.[1] Thickness is listed as 8.95 mm folded and 4.2 mm unfolded, excluding the display bezel and camera module.[1] Approximate weight including the battery is 237 grams for the standard HUAWEI Mate X8 and 239 grams for the Collector's Edition.[1]

Like earlier Mate X book-style models, the HUAWEI Mate X8 folds inward and retains a circular rear camera module.[4] TechRepublic and ShiftDelete.Net reported that Huawei introduced a magnetic flattening hinge incorporating a 0.1 mm magnetic composite and aerospace-grade steel laminate intended to reduce crease visibility on the inner display.[6][7] According to Huawei's launch presentation figures cited by ShiftDelete.Net, the company claims the hinge structure can keep crease height difference as low as 15 microns; independent third-party durability testing has not yet been published as of November 26, 2026.[7]

## HUAWEI Mate X8 inner and cover displays

The HUAWEI Mate X8 features an 8.2-inch foldable flexible OLED inner display with a resolution of 2424 x 2340 pixels (412 PPI), 1.07 billion colors, P3 wide color gamut, a 1 to 120 Hz LTPO adaptive refresh rate, 1440 Hz high-frequency PWM dimming, up to 240 Hz touch sampling, and ultra-thin glass (UTG) protection.[1]

The outer cover display is a 6.54-inch 3D quad-curved dual-layer OLED panel with a resolution of 2444 x 1132 pixels (412 PPI), 1 to 120 Hz LTPO adaptive refresh rate, 1440 Hz PWM dimming, up to 300 Hz touch sampling, and Basalt Kunlun Glass protection.[1] Launch materials cited by ShiftDelete.Net rate peak local brightness at up to 8,000 nits on the inner display and up to 12,000 nits on the outer display at 1% average picture level.[7]

## HUAWEI Mate X8 processor, cameras, and battery

The HUAWEI Mate X8 runs HarmonyOS 7.0 and is powered by the Kirin 9050 Pro processor across both the Standard and Collector's Edition models.[2][6][7] Memory is 12GB RAM on the standard model (with 256GB or 512GB storage) and 16GB RAM on the Collector's Edition (with 512GB, 1TB, or 2TB storage).[2][6]

TechRepublic reported that the main hardware difference between the two editions is a larger 50 MP main camera sensor on the Collector's Edition compared with the standard model's 50 MP main camera.[6] Power is supplied by a 6,200 mAh battery supporting 66W wired charging and 50W wireless charging.[5][6]
