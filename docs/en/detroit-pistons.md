## Detroit Pistons franchise overview

The Detroit Pistons are a professional basketball team in Detroit, Michigan, competing in 79 NBA seasons from 1948-49 through 2026-27.[6] Known earlier in franchise history as the Fort Wayne Pistons, the organization has made 44 playoff appearances and won three NBA championships.[6]

In the 2026-27 season, the Detroit Pistons are coached by J.B. Bickerstaff and compete in Emirates NBA Cup East Group A alongside regular-season league play.[1][2]

## Detroit Pistons 104-86 win over Orlando Magic

The Detroit Pistons defeated the Orlando Magic 104-86 on November 24, 2026, at Kia Center in Orlando, Florida, in an Emirates NBA Cup East Group A matchup.[1][5] The road victory improved the Detroit Pistons to 11-6 on the season while dropping Orlando to 7-10.[2][5]

Shortly before tip-off, Detroit learned that guard Cade Cunningham would be a late scratch due to right knee soreness, joining Jalen Duren and Duncan Robinson on the sidelined list.[2] Despite missing three starters, missing all six first-quarter three-point attempts, and trailing 22-16 after the opening quarter, Detroit leaned on physical defense to control the remainder of the contest.[2]

## Detroit Pistons key performers on November 24, 2026

The Detroit Pistons received balanced contributions across their reshuffled starting lineup and bench in the 104-86 victory over Orlando.[1][2] Isaiah Joe led Detroit in scoring with 21 points on 8-of-15 shooting, including 5-of-11 from three-point range, in 30 minutes of action.[1]

Daniss Jenkins recorded a double-double with 15 points and 10 assists, while Kevin Huerter scored 13 points with 6 rebounds and 5 assists and John Collins added 12 points and 2 blocks.[1] Forward Ausar Thompson anchored the defensive effort with 10 rebounds, 7 assists, 4 blocks, and 6 points on 3-of-3 shooting.[1]

## Detroit Pistons and Emirates NBA Cup implications

The Detroit Pistons victory over Orlando reshaped the East Group A race in the Emirates NBA Cup 2026.[3][4] According to NBA.com's November 24 recap, Orlando failed to clinch a Quarterfinals berth after losing to Detroit, keeping East Group A contention open entering the final games of Group Play.[3]

The Emirates NBA Cup 2026 features Group Play followed by Quarterfinals and a Championship game scheduled for Hinkle Fieldhouse.[4] Future Detroit Pistons active lineups and final East Group A standings remain subject to upcoming games not yet played as of 2026-11-25.[3]
