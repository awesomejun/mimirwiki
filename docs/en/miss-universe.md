# Miss Universe

> Miss Universe is an international beauty pageant run by the Miss Universe Organization. At the 75th edition on 2026-11-24 in San Juan, Puerto Rico, 27-year-old Jennifer Barreto of Puerto Rico won the crown among 116 contestants, becoming the first mother and married woman to win Miss Universe.

Miss Universe is an international beauty pageant operated by the Miss Universe Organization (MUO), bringing together delegates from countries and territories worldwide. On 2026-11-24, the 75th edition of Miss Universe (Miss Universe 2026) took place at the Coliseo de Puerto Rico Jose Miguel Agrelot in San Juan, Puerto Rico, with 116 contestants competing. Jennifer Barreto of Puerto Rico, 27, was crowned Miss Universe 2026 by outgoing titleholder Fatima Bosch of Mexico, becoming the first mother and married woman to win the title and delivering Puerto Rico's sixth Miss Universe crown.

## Miss Universe organization and eligibility

Miss Universe is an international beauty pageant operated by the Miss Universe Organization (MUO), which states on its official site that it provides an international platform for women across cultures, backgrounds, and religions.[1] Contestants represent countries and territories in preliminary stages including closed-door interviews, the national costume show, and preliminary evening gown and swimsuit competitions before advancing to the televised grand finale.[3][7]

Eligibility rules changed in 2023 to allow women with spouses or children to compete in Miss Universe.[7] According to Telemundo's 2026-11-25 release, Latin American contestants have won 28 Miss Universe crowns across the pageant's history.[4]

## Miss Universe 75th edition in Puerto Rico (2026)

Miss Universe held its 75th edition on Tuesday, 2026-11-24 at the Coliseo de Puerto Rico Jose Miguel Agrelot (known as El Choliseo, capacity 18,500) in San Juan, Puerto Rico.[2][4] MUO President Raul Rocha Cantu had announced Puerto Rico as the 75th-anniversary host on 2025-09-23.[2] Telemundo broadcast the finale live coast-to-coast starting at 7:00 p.m. ET (6:00 p.m. CT / 4:00 p.m. PT), hosted by Mario Lopez and former Miss Universe R'Bonney Gabriel.[3][7]

## Miss Universe 2026 winner Jennifer Barreto

Miss Universe 2026 winner Jennifer Barreto, 27, became the first mother and married woman to win the Miss Universe crown, according to Business Insider.[7] Her victory gave Puerto Rico its sixth Miss Universe title, following Marisol Malaret (1970), Deborah Carthy-Deu (1985), Dayanara Torres (1993), Denise Quinones (2001), and Zuleyka Rivera (2006).[2][5]

HOLA! reported that Barreto had never competed in a beauty pageant before entering Miss Universe Puerto Rico and has professional experience in psychology, social work, and prevention.[5] Puerto Rico Governor Jenniffer Gonzalez-Colon praised Barreto's win in an Instagram post.[7] Telemundo announced that Barreto will make her first major television appearance following her coronation during the live broadcast of the 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade on Thursday, 2026-11-26 at 8:30 a.m. ET on Telemundo (simulcast with NBC).[4]
