# Google Wallet

> On iPhone (launched in the U.S. on 2026-11-17), Google Wallet stores boarding passes, event tickets, loyalty and gym cards, receipts, package tracking from Gmail, and saved payment cards for online Google Pay checkout, but does not support in-store tap-to-pay.

Google Wallet is Google's digital wallet application for storing payment cards, boarding passes, event tickets, loyalty cards, receipts, and delivery updates across Google services. On 2026-11-17, Google launched Google Wallet on iOS in the United States, enabling iPhone users to organize passes, track orders, and manage saved cards for online Google Pay checkout, while in-store contactless tap-to-pay remains unsupported on iOS.

## Google Wallet overview and core features

Google Wallet is a digital wallet service from Google that stores payment cards, loyalty passes, event tickets, boarding passes, receipts, and delivery tracking in one application.[2] On Android devices, Google Wallet supports contactless tap-to-pay wherever Google Pay is accepted and requires screen lock verification using a PIN, pattern, password, or Class 3 biometric unlock before completing payments.[2][3]

## Google Wallet November 2026 U.S. iOS launch

Google Wallet became available on iOS in the United States on November 17, 2026, in an announcement published by P.J. Linarducci, Vice President of Consumer Payments at Google.[1][4] HiTech Reports noted on November 25, 2026, that it is Google's first standalone digital wallet app on iOS since the Google Pay app was discontinued in 2024.[6]

On iPhone, Google Wallet syncs with Gmail and other Google apps to organize digital boarding passes, event tickets, receipts, and package delivery updates, and lets users add loyalty and gym membership cards.[2][4] Google also stated that the app allows users to manage 'agentic purchases made across Google' with order confirmations, receipts, and delivery updates, while Payments Dive noted the launch positions Google among iPhone-based agentic shoppers.[4][5]

## Google Wallet payment capabilities and tap-to-pay limits on iPhone

Google Wallet on iPhone can store payment cards for online checkout with Google Pay across Google services, but it cannot be used for in-store contactless tap-to-pay at retail or transit terminals.[2][4][6] Google's official product page explicitly states that in-store tap-to-pay is not supported on iOS at this time.[2]

As of November 26, 2026, Google has not announced a timeline for bringing in-store tap-to-pay to iOS or for expanding the iOS app to countries outside the United States.[6][7]

## Google Wallet security and verification on Android

Google Wallet enforces device-level security on Android by requiring a screen lock before users can complete payments with saved payment methods.[3] Google Wallet Help lists PIN, pattern, password, and Class 3 biometric unlock as supported verification methods, noting that users must verify again once a verification session times out.[3]

## Frequently asked questions

### When did Google Wallet launch on iPhone in the United States?

Google announced the U.S. iOS launch of Google Wallet on November 17, 2026. [1][4]

### What can iPhone users store in Google Wallet?

iPhone users can store digital boarding passes, event tickets, loyalty and gym membership cards, receipts, package tracking synced from Gmail and other Google apps, and saved payment cards for online Google Pay checkout. [2][4]

### Does Google Wallet support in-store tap-to-pay on iPhone?

No. Google's official Wallet site and launch coverage state that in-store contactless tap-to-pay is not supported on iOS at this time, and no schedule for adding it has been announced. [2][6]

### How does Google Wallet verify payments on Android devices?

According to Google Wallet Help, Android users must set up a screen lock and verify their identity using a PIN, pattern, password, or Class 3 biometric unlock to make payments. [3]
