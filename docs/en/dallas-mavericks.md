## Dallas Mavericks overview and 2026-27 standings

The Dallas Mavericks are a Dallas-based NBA franchise competing in the Western Conference and placed in West Group A for the Emirates NBA Cup 2026.[2][7] Following their 2026-11-24 road win in Denver, Dallas improved to 11-7 overall (eighth in the Western Conference) with a season-best four-game winning streak, while Denver dropped to 10-6 and saw its three-game winning streak end.[1][7]

In NBA Cup West Group A, both Dallas and Denver entered 2-0; with the win, the Mavericks moved to 3-0 with a +36 point differential.[4][5] Dallas can clinch West Group A and a Knockout Round berth with either a Phoenix loss to Houston on Wednesday, 2026-11-25 or a Mavericks victory at Phoenix on Friday, 2026-11-27.[1][3][4] Sports Illustrated noted that while Dallas reached the 2024 knockout round as a wild card (losing to Oklahoma City), winning West Group A would be the franchise's first group title.[5]

## Dallas Mavericks 120-101 win over Denver Nuggets (2026-11-24)

The Dallas Mavericks defeated the Denver Nuggets 120-101 on Tuesday, 2026-11-24 at Ball Arena in Denver.[1][2] Dallas led by 19 points at halftime and by as many as 22 overall; although Denver cut the deficit to 80-77 with 1:11 left in the third quarter, the Mavericks rebuilt their lead in the fourth quarter.[4][6]

Dallas outscored Denver 68-38 in points in the paint and 35-7 in fast-break points, holding the Nuggets to 40.5% shooting compared with Denver's NBA-leading 49% entering the game.[1][4][6]

## Dallas Mavericks key performances on 2026-11-24

Dallas Mavericks guard Cooper Flagg recorded 24 points (8-of-17 shooting, 7-of-9 free throws), 12 assists (tying a career high), and six rebounds in 31:27.[2][4] CBS Sports noted it was Flagg's 11th 20-point double-double, tying Dwight Howard for the second-most by a teenager in NBA history behind LeBron James (18) ahead of Flagg's 20th birthday on 2026-12-21.[4]
