# Google Gemini

> Developed by Google DeepMind, Google Gemini is a natively multimodal AI model series and product ecosystem spanning consumer subscriptions (Google AI Plus, Pro, and Ultra) and developer platforms (Google AI Studio and Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform). As of November 2026, the flagship Gemini 4 family comprises Gemini 4 Carbon, Gemini 4 Neon, Gemini 4 Argon, and Gemini 4 Hydrogen, each supporting a 1-million-token context window, up to 64K/1M output tokens across text, image, audio, video, and PDF inputs, and promotional paid API rates of $2.00 input / $10.00 output per million tokens for Carbon and Argon through November 30, 2026 ($4.00/$20.00 starting December 1, 2026).

Google Gemini is the flagship family of natively multimodal artificial intelligence foundation models and assistant applications developed by Google DeepMind and Alphabet Inc. First introduced in December 2023 with Gemini 1.0 and expanded through Gemini 1.5, 2.5, 3, and the chemical-element-named Gemini 4 generation (`gemini-4-carbon`, `gemini-4-neon`, `gemini-4-argon`, and `gemini-4-hydrogen`), Gemini powers all 15 of Google's half-billion-user products (including seven products with over two billion users) as well as Google AI Studio, the Gemini Developer API, and the Google Cloud Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform.

**Also known as:** Google Gemini, Google DeepMind, Gemini 4 Carbon, Gemini 4 Neon, Multimodal AI, Google AI Studio

## Evolution from Gemini 1.0 to Gemini 4 and Alphabet Ecosystem Scale

Google DeepMind introduced the Gemini architecture in December 2023 (`arXiv:2312.11805`, submitted December 19, 2023) as a family of models trained jointly from the ground up across image, audio, video, and text modalities.[7] In March 2024, Google DeepMind published Gemini 1.5 (`arXiv:2403.05530`, submitted March 8, 2024), unlocking long-context multimodal understanding across millions of tokens of context.[8] This was followed in July 2025 by Gemini 2.5 (`arXiv:2507.06261`, submitted July 7, 2025), which integrated thinking tokens, advanced reasoning, and next-generation agentic capabilities, and subsequently by the Gemini 3 generation highlighted in Alphabet Inc.'s FY2025 Form 10-K (filed February 5, 2026).[1][9]

According to Alphabet Inc.'s Form 10-K filing with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, Gemini models are embedded across all 15 of Google's products serving more than 500 million users, including seven products with more than two billion users, as well as enterprise offerings such as Gemini Enterprise and Gemini for Google Workspace.[1] By November 2026, Google DeepMind accelerated its frontier release cadence with the Gemini 4 family, transitioning from traditional size labels to chemical-element designations (`Carbon`, `Neon`, `Argon`, and `Hydrogen`).[3][10]

## Gemini 4 and Specialized Gemini 3 Model Catalog

On the Gemini Developer API and Google AI Studio, the current model catalog is organized into the flagship Gemini 4 series (where every variant shares a 1,000,000-token context window, a 64,000 / 1,000,000-token maximum output limit, and native support for Text, Image, Audio, Video, and PDF modalities) alongside specialized Gemini 3.x and interactive media endpoints:[3]

| Model Name | API Endpoint Code | Tier & Primary Workload Profile |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gemini 4 Carbon | `gemini-4-carbon` | Strongest frontier reasoning model; uses extended verification loops and thought tokens for complex software engineering, cybersecurity defense, deep math/science, and 1M-token long-decode generation |
| Gemini 4 Neon | `gemini-4-neon` | Frontier-level intelligence optimized for fast end-to-end task completion across software engineering, multimodal knowledge work, and scientific workflows |
| Gemini 4 Argon | `gemini-4-argon` | Efficient frontier reasoning model delivering Gemini 4 intelligence with faster responses and lower token spend per turn than Carbon |
| Gemini 4 Hydrogen | `gemini-4-hydrogen` | High-speed, cost-efficient Gemini 4 model designed for everyday coding, high-throughput tasks, and agentic workflows |
| Gemini 3.8 Flash | `gemini-3.8-flash` | Stable previous-generation Flash model for software engineering and autonomous enterprise workflows |
| Gemini 3.8 Live & Extended Thinking | `gemini-3.8-live` / `gemini-3.8-live-extended-thinking` | Low-latency Live API voice agent models (standard and high-background-reasoning variants) |
| Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS & Flash-Lite TTS | `gemini-3.8-flash-tts` / `gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts` | Studio-grade and high-volume text-to-speech models supporting expressive acting, voice design, and voice replication |
| Nano Banana 2.1 / 2 / Pro | `gemini-nano-banana-2.1` / `gemini-3.1-flash-image` / `gemini-3-pro-image` | Native image generation and editing models optimized for typography, multi-turn consistency, and high-volume visual creation |
| Gemini Omni Flash | `gemini-omni-flash` (`gemini-omni-1.2-flash`) | Video generation and editing model creating up to 15 seconds of video with native audio, keyframe interpolation, video extension, and character consistency |
| Genie 4 | `genie-4` | Real-time interactive world model streaming steerable 24 fps environments with synchronized 48 kHz stereo audio over WebRTC and WebSockets |
| Gemini Decision 1 | `gemini-decision-1` | High-throughput decision model served on the Decisions API (up to 15,000 RPM and 10M TPM at Tier 3) |

The catalog also maintains stable endpoints for `gemini-3.6-flash`, `gemini-3.5-flash-lite`, `gemini-3.1-flash-lite`, `gemini-3.5-transcribe` (low-latency speech-to-text with utterance-level language detection and speaker diarization), and `gemini-3.5-live-translate-preview` (real-time speech-to-speech translation across 70+ languages).[3]

## Gemini Developer API Pricing, Usage Tiers, and Rate Limits

On the Gemini Developer API, Google offers a Free tier (available on select models where submitted content may be used to improve Google products) and a Paid tier where customer data is not used to train models.[4] Neither `gemini-4-carbon` nor `gemini-4-argon` is available on the Free tier, and every token processed by those models is billed, including internal thinking tokens and tool system instructions or function declarations when using Grounding with Google Search (which includes 5,000 free search requests per month on paid tiers and a default project quota of 1,500 requests per day).[4][5]

For both `gemini-4-carbon` and `gemini-4-argon`, Google instituted promotional launch pricing through November 30, 2026, before transitioning to standard list rates on December 1, 2026:[4]

- **Input Price (Paid Tier)**: $2.00 per 1M tokens through November 30, 2026; $4.00 per 1M tokens starting December 1, 2026.[4]
- **Output Price (including thinking tokens)**: $10.00 per 1M tokens through November 30, 2026; $20.00 per 1M tokens starting December 1, 2026.[4]
- **Context Caching Price (Implicit caching only)**: $0.10 per 1M tokens through November 30, 2026; $0.20 per 1M tokens starting December 1, 2026 (explicit caching is not supported on Carbon and Argon).[4]

Gemini API rate limits are enforced per Google Cloud project (rather than per API key), with daily request quotas (RPD) resetting at midnight Pacific time.[5] In addition to RPM, TPM, and RPD quotas, Google enforces a rolling 10-minute spend-based rate limit (`429 RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED`) tied to the project's cumulative Google Cloud billing history:[5]

| Usage Tier | Qualification Criteria | Monthly Billing Tier Cap | Rolling 10-Min Spend Limit | Gemini Omni Flash (RPM / RPD) | Genie 4 (Concurrent Sessions / RPD) | Gemini Decision 1 (RPM / TPM / RPD) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | Active project or free trial | N/A | N/A | Not supported | Not supported | 200 / 50K / 10,000 |
| Tier 1 | Linked active billing account | $250 | $10 / 10 min | 8 / 40 | 2 / 50 | 1,500 / 1M / 50,000 |
| Tier 2 | $100+ cumulative spend + 3 days since first payment | $2,000 | $200 / 10 min | 16 / 200 | 5 / 250 | 5,000 / 4M / Unlimited |
| Tier 3 | $1,000+ cumulative spend + 30 days since first payment | $20,000 - $100,000+ | $200 / 10 min | 40 / 2,000 | 10 / 1,000 | 15,000 / 10M / Unlimited |

On the Interactions API for `genie-4`, real-time in-session control messages (`user_action` and mid-session `input` events) do not consume separate RPM or RPD quota, though Genie 4 does not support the Batch API, Flex inference, or Priority inference.[5] Developers in unsupported Google AI Studio regions can access Gemini models through the Google Cloud Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform.[6]

## Google One AI Plans and Workspace Integration

For consumers, creators, and independent developers, Google distributes Gemini capabilities and Google One cloud storage through three tiered subscriptions (all supporting family sharing with up to five additional members):[2]

1. **Google AI Plus (400 GB Storage)**: Includes access to Gemini 4 Hydrogen, Deep Research, Tasks in Gemini, Chrome auto browse, Gemini Notebook, proofreading in Gmail, and Gmail Live on Android and iOS (US only).[2] 2. **Google AI Pro (5 TB Storage)**: Upgrades the user to 4x more Gemini access featuring Gemini 4 Neon in the Gemini app, Google Antigravity, and Google AI Studio, plus 200 Google Colab compute units per month, $10 in monthly Google Cloud credits via the Google Developer Program, expanded agentic AI assistance in Android Studio, Gemini in Docs/Sheets and Docs Live (US only), Google Flow video creation, YouTube Premium Lite (individual), Google Health Premium, and Google Home Premium Standard (30-day event history, $10/month value).[2] Verified students receive Google AI Pro free for one year or bundled with full YouTube Premium for $8.99/month.[2] 3. **Google AI Ultra (Starting at 20 TB Storage, available in 150+ countries)**: Provides 5x higher usage limits than AI Pro and unlocks Gemini 4 Carbon across the Gemini app and AI Mode in Search, interactive real-time world generation with Project Genie, 2,000 Google Colab compute units per month, $40 in monthly Google Cloud credits, the YouTube Premium Family plan (in 100+ countries), Google Health Premium, and Google Home Premium Advanced (24/7 event history and descriptions, $20/month value).[2]

Across the Gemini application ecosystem, Deep Research autonomously browses and synthesizes hundreds of websites in real time into multi-page research reports, while Tasks in Gemini executes background multi-step web workflows on the user's behalf.[2]

## Frequently asked questions

### What are the differences between Gemini 4 Carbon, Neon, Argon, and Hydrogen?

All four Gemini 4 models support a 1-million-token context window, 64K/1M max output tokens, and text, image, audio, video, and PDF inputs. Gemini 4 Carbon is Google's strongest frontier reasoning model, using extended verification loops and thought tokens for the hardest software engineering, cybersecurity, and scientific problems. Gemini 4 Neon delivers frontier-level intelligence optimized for fast end-to-end task completion. Gemini 4 Argon is an efficient frontier reasoning variant that lowers token spend and latency per turn compared to Carbon, while Gemini 4 Hydrogen is a high-speed, cost-efficient model for everyday coding and high-volume agentic workflows. [3]

### How much do Gemini 4 Carbon and Gemini 4 Argon cost on the Gemini Developer API?

Neither Gemini 4 Carbon nor Gemini 4 Argon is offered on the Free tier. On the Paid tier, both models share promotional pricing through November 30, 2026 of $2.00 per million input tokens, $10.00 per million output tokens (including thinking tokens), and $0.10 per million implicit cached tokens. Starting December 1, 2026, standard pricing for both models increases to $4.00 per million input tokens, $20.00 per million output tokens, and $0.20 per million implicit cached tokens. [4]

### Which Google One AI subscription plan includes Gemini 4 Carbon and Gemini 4 Neon?

Google AI Plus (400 GB storage) includes Gemini 4 Hydrogen. Google AI Pro (5 TB storage) unlocks Gemini 4 Neon across the Gemini app, Google Antigravity, and Google AI Studio, along with 200 monthly Colab compute units and $10 in monthly Google Cloud credits. Google AI Ultra (starting at 20 TB storage) unlocks Gemini 4 Carbon in the Gemini app and AI Mode in Search, 5x higher usage limits than AI Pro, Project Genie interactive worlds, 2,000 monthly Colab compute units, $40 in monthly Google Cloud credits, and a YouTube Premium Family plan. [2]

### How do usage tiers and spend-based rate limits work on the Gemini API?

Gemini API rate limits are applied per Google Cloud project across RPM, TPM, and RPD (resetting at midnight Pacific time), plus a rolling 10-minute spend limit. Linking a billing account unlocks Tier 1 ($250 monthly cap, $10 per 10-minute spend limit); $100 in cumulative Google Cloud spend plus 3 days unlocks Tier 2 ($2,000 monthly cap, $200 per 10-minute spend limit); and $1,000 in cumulative spend plus 30 days unlocks Tier 3 ($20,000 to $100,000+ monthly cap, $200 per 10-minute spend limit). [5]
