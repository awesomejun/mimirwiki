## Analog Devices corporate overview

Analog Devices, Inc. (Nasdaq: ADI) is a Massachusetts-incorporated global semiconductor manufacturer headquartered in Wilmington, Massachusetts.[1][2] Led by CEO and Chair Vincent Roche and CFO Richard Puccio, the company supplies semiconductor solutions across industrial, automotive, communications, data center, and consumer markets.[2][6]

## Analog Devices Q4 and fiscal 2026 financial results

Analog Devices reported record financial results for its fiscal fourth quarter and full fiscal year 2026 (ended October 31, 2026) in a Form 8-K and Exhibit 99.1 filed with the SEC on November 24, 2026.[1][2][4] Fourth-quarter revenue rose 42 percent year over year to $4.37 billion, with GAAP diluted EPS of $3.40 and adjusted diluted EPS of $4.06.[2][5]

Full-year fiscal 2026 revenue reached a record $15.2 billion, up 38 percent from fiscal 2025.[2] Operating cash flow totaled $6.2 billion (41 percent of revenue) and free cash flow totaled $5.5 billion (36 percent of revenue), while the company returned $5.4 billion to shareholders through dividends and share repurchases.[2]

## Analog Devices Data Center and Industrial revenue drivers

Analog Devices stated in its November 24, 2026 SEC filing that year-over-year growth in the fourth quarter was led by Data Center and Industrial demand.[2] The company's Form 10-K and earnings call materials also detailed broad demand across industrial, automotive, communications, and consumer end markets.[3][6]

CEO Vincent Roche noted that while AI infrastructure investments drove strong growth across data center and automated test equipment (ATE) businesses, more than two-thirds of Analog Devices' fiscal 2026 growth came from non-AI-centric market opportunities.[2]

## Analog Devices Q1 fiscal 2027 outlook and open uncertainties

In its Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1, Analog Devices issued first-quarter fiscal 2027 guidance of $4.4 billion in revenue (plus or minus $150 million), reported operating margin of approximately 40.3% (plus or minus 150 bps), adjusted operating margin of approximately 50.5% (plus or minus 100 bps), reported EPS of $3.02 (plus or minus $0.20), and adjusted EPS of $3.82 (plus or minus $0.20).[2] Financial press coverage noted market focus on near-term earnings guidance following the release.[5]

Actual first-quarter fiscal 2027 revenue and whether segment growth rates across Data Center and Industrial will be sustained remain unverified as of November 26, 2026.[2]
