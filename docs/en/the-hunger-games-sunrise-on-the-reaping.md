## The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping overview and source novel

The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping is the sixth feature film in Lionsgate's Hunger Games film franchise, released in theaters on November 20, 2026.[1] It adapts Suzanne Collins' fifth Hunger Games novel, Sunrise on the Reaping, which Scholastic originally published on March 18, 2025.[1]

Ahead of the film's theatrical release, Scholastic announced trade paperback and movie tie-in editions of the novel in January 2026, reporting that the book had sold more than 4.4 million World English copies across print, e-books, and audiobooks.[1][2]

## The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping story and franchise connection

The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping is the second prequel in the film series, following 2023's The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes.[5][6] Set 24 years before the events of the original Hunger Games trilogy, the film depicts the 50th Hunger Games and tells the origin story of District 12 victor Haymitch Abernathy.[4][7]

In the original trilogy, Haymitch Abernathy was portrayed as a middle-aged mentor to Katniss and Peeta by Woody Harrelson, whereas Sunrise on the Reaping centers on a young Haymitch played by Joseph Zada.[3][7]

## The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping opening box office

The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping opened over the November 20-22, 2026 weekend to an estimated $225 million worldwide, including $120 million in North America and $105 million across 84 international territories.[3] Media Play News reported that the film played on 10,475 North American screens and roughly 40,000 overseas screens, finishing first in 81 international markets.[3]

TheWrap reported that the $120 million domestic launch across 4,018 locations was Lionsgate's first $100 million-plus opening since The Hunger Games: Mockingjay - Part 2 ($102 million unadjusted) in 2015, and ranked among Lionsgate's top five domestic openings of all time.[4] Thursday previews and early-access screenings generated $21 million, more than triple the $5.75 million previews of The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes in November 2023.[6] The film added $12.8 million on its first Monday to reach $132.8 million domestically, and audiences awarded it an A- CinemaScore.[4][5]

## The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping theatrical run status

The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping remains in the early stage of its theatrical run as of November 25, 2026, entering the Thanksgiving holiday week after its opening weekend and first Monday.[5][6] Final worldwide box office totals and post-theatrical streaming or home-video release dates have not been announced as of November 25, 2026.[3][5]
