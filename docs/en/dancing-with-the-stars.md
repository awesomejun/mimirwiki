## Dancing with the Stars format and broadcast

Dancing with the Stars is an unscripted dance competition series produced in front of a live studio audience by BBC Studios and broadcast on ABC and Disney+, with episodes available to stream the next day on Hulu.[1] Celebrity contestants pair with professional dancers and perform routines evaluated by a judging panel, with competition outcomes determined by combining judges' scores and viewer votes.[5][6] Winners receive the Len Goodman Mirrorball Trophy.[1][2]

For the Season 35 finale on Tuesday, 2026-11-24, ABC announced a three-hour broadcast airing live in every U.S. time zone (8:00 to 11:00 p.m. EST, 7:00 to 10:00 p.m. CST, 6:00 to 9:00 p.m. MST, and 5:00 to 8:00 p.m. PST) for the first time in the show's history, followed by a Pacific Time re-airing.[1]

## Dancing with the Stars first Paralympian winner

Dancing with the Stars crowned Ezra Frech as the first Paralympian to win the U.S. edition of the competition on 2026-11-24.[5] Born with congenital limb differences and using a prosthetic leg from an early age, the 21-year-old athlete won gold medals in the men's 100m T63 and men's high jump T63 at the Paris 2024 Paralympic Games, as well as the Paris 2023 world title in the high jump T63.[3][4]

In a 2026-11-25 appearance on *Good Morning America* alongside Karagach, Frech explained that they began their Freestyle dance without his prosthetic leg as a creative choice, encountered a suction failure mid-routine when putting it on, and finished the dance after reattaching it.[2] During the same broadcast, Frech credited messages from the disabled community as his "fuel" throughout Season 35.[3]
