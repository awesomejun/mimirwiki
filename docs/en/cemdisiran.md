# Cemdisiran (Livsayis)

> On November 18, 2026, the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) approved Livsayis (cemdisiran, NDA 221251) under Priority Review for the treatment of generalized myasthenia gravis (gMG) in adult patients who are anti-acetylcholine receptor (AChR) antibody-positive, making it the first FDA-approved siRNA therapy for gMG and the seventh FDA-approved medicine originating from Alnylam's RNAi platform. Approval was supported by the Phase 3 NIMBLE trial (NCT05070858), where cemdisiran 600 mg monotherapy every 12 weeks reduced week-24 MG-ADL total score by a placebo-adjusted 2.3 points (p = 0.0005). Because complement inhibition increases susceptibility to Neisseria meningitidis, Livsayis carries a Boxed Warning for serious meningococcal infections and is dispensed exclusively through the restricted Livsayis REMS program.

Cemdisiran (marketed under the brand name Livsayis) is a complement component 5 (C5)-directed small interfering RNA (siRNA) therapeutic discovered by Alnylam Pharmaceuticals and developed and commercialized by Regeneron Pharmaceuticals. Administered as a quarterly (every 12 weeks) subcutaneous injection, it uses RNA interference (RNAi) to silence hepatic C5 messenger RNA (mRNA), reducing circulating C5 protein levels and inhibiting terminal complement-mediated damage at the neuromuscular junction.

**Also known as:** Livsayis, ALN-CC5, NDA 221251

## Indication and November 2026 FDA Approval

**Cemdisiran** (brand name **Livsayis**, pronounced *liv-SAY-iss*) is a prescription RNA interference (RNAi) therapeutic indicated for the treatment of **generalized myasthenia gravis (gMG)** in adult patients who are **anti-acetylcholine receptor (AChR) antibody-positive** [1][2][3].

On **November 18, 2026**, the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) approved New Drug Application **NDA 221251** under Priority Review (listed as Novel Drug Therapy Approval #55 of 2026 by the FDA Center for Drug Evaluation and Research) [1][2]. Cemdisiran was discovered by **Alnylam Pharmaceuticals, Inc.** (Nasdaq: `ALNY`) and advanced into Phase 2 development by Alnylam before becoming part of an RNAi collaboration with **Regeneron Pharmaceuticals, Inc.** [5]. In 2024, Regeneron assumed sole responsibility for development, manufacturing, and commercialization [5]. Upon FDA approval on November 18, 2026, Livsayis became the first FDA-approved siRNA therapy for myasthenia gravis, Regeneron's first approved siRNA and neurology medicine, and the seventh FDA-approved therapeutic originating from Alnylam's RNAi platform (under its agreement with Regeneron, Alnylam will receive a $25 million payment for the approval and is eligible for tiered, double-digit royalties of up to 15% on global net sales) [5][7].

## Mechanism of Action and Quarterly Subcutaneous Dosing

Generalized myasthenia gravis is a chronic autoimmune neuromuscular disease in which the immune system produces antibodies that disrupt communication between nerves and muscles at the neuromuscular junction, causing muscle weakness [6]. In anti-acetylcholine receptor (AChR) antibody-positive gMG, activation of the complement system contributes to damage at the neuromuscular junction [5].

Rather than blocking the C5 protein itself, cemdisiran is a **small interfering RNA (siRNA)** that inhibits C5 synthesis in the liver [7]. It degrades the messenger RNA (mRNA) encoding **complement component 5 (C5)**, lowering circulating C5 and inhibiting terminal complement activation [5][7].

Livsayis is supplied as a **189 mg/mL** solution in a single-dose vial for subcutaneous injection [1][3]. The Medication Guide states that a healthcare provider gives a total dose of **567 mg**, as 2 separate subcutaneous injections, **every 3 months** [3]. The Phase 3 trial tested cemdisiran 600 mg every 12 weeks [7]. Livsayis is the only FDA-approved gMG treatment administered subcutaneously four times a year [6][7].

## Pivotal Phase 3 NIMBLE Trial (NCT05070858)

The FDA approval of Livsayis was based on efficacy and safety data from the pivotal Phase 3 **NIMBLE** study (`NCT05070858`), a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial conducted across 86 clinical centers in 13 countries (initiated December 14, 2021, with primary completion on July 8, 2025) [4][7].

While the broader NIMBLE protocol enrolled 288 symptomatic gMG adults (with 284 randomized across cemdisiran monotherapy, cemdisiran plus the C5 antibody pozelimab, pozelimab monotherapy, or placebo on top of permitted background immunosuppressants), the FDA approval specifically evaluated the **cemdisiran 600 mg every-12-week monotherapy arm** ($N = 79$) compared against placebo through Week 24 [4][7]:

| NIMBLE Efficacy Endpoint (Week 24) | Cemdisiran 600 mg q12w | Placebo | Placebo-Adjusted Difference & Significance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Primary Endpoint: Change in MG-ADL Total Score** (0-24 scale) | **-4.5 points** (LS mean) | **-2.2 points** (LS mean) | **-2.3 points** (95% CI: -3.6 to -1.0; $p = 0.0005$) [7] |
| **MG-ADL Responder Rate** ($\ge 3$-point improvement) | **76.6%** | **44.1%** | Statistically significant ($p < 0.001$) [7] |
| **Key Secondary Endpoint: Change in QMG Total Score** | **-4.2 points** | **-1.5 points** | **-2.8 points** difference ($p = 0.0015$) [7] |

According to Regeneron, improvements emerged as early as **Week 2** and were sustained through Week 24 [7]. Although NIMBLE also enrolled patients with anti-LRP4 antibodies, the FDA-approved indication is restricted to **anti-AChR antibody-positive** adults [2][7].

## Boxed Warning, Meningococcal Risk, and Livsayis REMS

Livsayis carries a **Boxed Warning** for **serious meningococcal infections** caused by *Neisseria meningitidis* [3][7]. The Medication Guide states that meningococcal infections may quickly become life-threatening or cause death if not recognized and treated early, and Livsayis is contraindicated in patients with a serious meningococcal infection at the start of treatment [3][7].

Key mandatory safety protocols in the label include [3][7]: - **Vaccination and Prophylaxis**: Patients must complete or update meningococcal vaccinations (covering serogroups A, C, W, Y, and B) **at least 2 weeks prior** to receiving their first dose of Livsayis. If urgent therapy must be initiated in an unvaccinated patient, required vaccines must be administered as soon as possible alongside **prophylactic antibacterial coverage** [3][7]. - **Encapsulated Bacteria Precautions**: The label also warns of increased susceptibility to infections by other encapsulated pathogens, including *Streptococcus pneumoniae*, *Haemophilus influenzae*, and *Neisseria gonorrhoeae* [7]. - **Livsayis REMS Program**: Livsayis is available only through a restricted distribution program under a Risk Evaluation and Mitigation Strategy (**LIVSAYIS REMS**, `www.LIVSAYISREMS.com`) [3]. Prescribers must enroll and counsel patients using the Patient Guide, patients must carry a **Patient Safety Card** at all times, and pharmacies must be certified to verify vaccination and prophylaxis compliance prior to dispensing [3][7].

## Frequently asked questions

### What is cemdisiran (Livsayis) approved for?

Livsayis (cemdisiran) was approved by the U.S. FDA on November 18, 2026 for the treatment of generalized myasthenia gravis (gMG) in adult patients who are anti-acetylcholine receptor (AChR) antibody-positive. [1][2][3]

### How does cemdisiran differ from other complement inhibitors used in myasthenia gravis?

Rather than blocking the C5 protein itself, cemdisiran is a small interfering RNA (siRNA) therapy that degrades the mRNA encoding C5 and inhibits C5 synthesis in the liver. It is given subcutaneously four times a year, and it is the only FDA-approved gMG treatment on that schedule. [5][6][7]

### What were the results of the Phase 3 NIMBLE trial for cemdisiran?

In the Phase 3 NIMBLE trial (NCT05070858), cemdisiran 600 mg subcutaneously every 12 weeks reduced the Myasthenia Gravis-Activities of Daily Living (MG-ADL) total score at Week 24 by 4.5 points compared with 2.2 points for placebo (a placebo-adjusted improvement of -2.3 points; p = 0.0005), with 76.6% of cemdisiran-treated patients achieving at least a 3-point MG-ADL improvement versus 44.1% on placebo. [4][7]

### What is the Boxed Warning and REMS requirement for Livsayis?

Livsayis carries a Boxed Warning for serious meningococcal infections caused by Neisseria meningitidis. Patients must complete meningococcal vaccinations at least 2 weeks before starting Livsayis (or receive prophylactic antibiotics if started urgently), carry a Patient Safety Card, and receive the drug exclusively through certified providers and pharmacies under the Livsayis REMS program. [3][7]

### Which companies developed cemdisiran (Livsayis)?

Cemdisiran was discovered by Alnylam Pharmaceuticals and advanced into Phase 2 development before becoming part of an RNAi collaboration with Regeneron Pharmaceuticals. Regeneron assumed sole responsibility for development, manufacturing and commercialization in 2024 and holds the FDA approval (NDA 221251). Alnylam will receive a $25 million payment for the approval and is eligible for tiered royalties of up to 15% on global net sales. [1][5]
