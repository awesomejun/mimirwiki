## Insmed Incorporated corporate profile

Insmed Incorporated is a Virginia-incorporated global biopharmaceutical company with principal executive offices at 700 US Highway 202/206, Bridgewater, New Jersey.[1][3] Its common stock ($0.01 par value per share) is registered on The Nasdaq Stock Market LLC (The Nasdaq Global Select Market) under the trading symbol `INSM`, with SEC CIK `0001104506` and Commission File Number `000-30739`.[1]

As of 2026-11-24, Will Lewis serves as Chair and Chief Executive Officer of Insmed.[4][6] The company's commercial portfolio consists of two approved respiratory medicines, Brinsupri and Arikayce, alongside clinical-stage and earlier-stage programs in respiratory, immunology, and rare diseases.[4][6]

## Insmed Incorporated and Sanofi merger agreement

Insmed Incorporated entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated 2026-11-23 with Sanofi, a French societe anonyme, and Polaris Merger Sub, Inc., a Delaware corporation and wholly owned subsidiary of Sanofi, and the two companies publicly announced the agreement on 2026-11-24.[1][2][4] Under the agreement, Polaris Merger Sub, Inc. will merge with and into Insmed, with Insmed surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of Sanofi.[1] At the effective time of the merger, each issued and outstanding share of Insmed common stock (excluding shares held by Sanofi, Merger Sub, or wholly owned subsidiaries of Insmed, Sanofi, or Merger Sub) will be converted into the right to receive $175.00 in cash, without interest and subject to any tax withholding.[1] Sanofi's announcement and accompanying investor presentation state that this reflects a total consideration and equity value of approximately $41.0 billion (EUR 37.6 billion) in cash.[4][5]

As of 2026-11-25, the acquisition is at the signed-agreement stage and has not closed.[1][4] Both companies' boards of directors approved the transaction, and Insmed's board resolved to recommend that shareholders approve the merger agreement and related plan of merger.[1][4] Completion of the merger is subject to customary closing conditions, including approval by the affirmative vote of more than two-thirds of all votes entitled to be cast by holders of Insmed common stock, expiration or termination of the waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976, receipt of certain foreign antitrust and foreign direct investment clearances, and the absence of prohibiting laws or orders.[1] The merger is not subject to a financing condition. Sanofi stated that it is funding the acquisition through a combination of debt financing and cash on hand, and the Form 8-K states that Sanofi's debt financing commitments include bridge facilities.[1][4]

The 2026-11-24 announcement states that, subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the closing conditions, the transaction is expected to close in the first half of 2027.[4] The merger agreement sets an initial outside termination date of 2027-08-23 at 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time (with automatic extensions to 2027-11-23 and 2028-02-23 under specified circumstances) and requires Insmed to pay a $1,125,000,000 cash termination fee under specified circumstances, such as a board recommendation change or termination to enter into a superior proposal.[1] Sanofi CEO Belen Garijo and Insmed Chair and CEO Will Lewis issued statements in the 2026-11-24 announcement.[4]

## Insmed Incorporated approved products and clinical pipeline

Insmed Incorporated markets two commercialized respiratory therapies and is advancing a Phase 3 clinical program, as disclosed in its 2026 filings and announcements.[4][6]

- **Brinsupri (brensocatib)**: A small-molecule, once-daily, oral, reversible inhibitor of dipeptidyl peptidase 1 (DPP1).[4] In the United States and Japan, Brinsupri is approved for the treatment of non-cystic fibrosis bronchiectasis (NCFB) in adults and pediatric patients aged 12 years and older; in the European Union and United Kingdom, it is approved for patients with NCFB aged 12 years and older who had two or more exacerbations in the prior 12 months.[4] Following its U.S. launch in August 2025, Japan's Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (MHLW) approved Brinsupri Tablets 25 mg (brensocatib hydrate) in August 2026, and Insmed launched it in Japan in October 2026.[4][6] Sanofi's release notes that Brinsupri is associated with dermatologic adverse reactions (including rash, dry skin, and hyperkeratosis) and gingival and periodontal adverse reactions.[4]
- **Arikayce (amikacin liposome inhalation suspension)**: An inhaled antibacterial treatment used in combination with multidrug therapy for certain adults with *Mycobacterium avium* complex (MAC) lung disease, approved for treatment-refractory patients in the United States, European Union, and Japan.[4] In September 2026, the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) accepted Insmed's Supplemental New Drug Application (sNDA) for Arikayce under Priority Review for the treatment of MAC lung disease as part of a combination antibacterial drug regimen.[4][6]
- **TPIP (treprostinil palmitil inhalation powder)**: A prostanoid in Phase 3 clinical development as a once-daily inhaled therapy for pulmonary hypertension and fibrotic lung disease, alongside earlier-stage programs in immunology and rare diseases.[4][6]
