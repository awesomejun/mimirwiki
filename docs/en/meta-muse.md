# Meta Muse

> Meta Muse is a personal AI agent introduced by Meta in September 2026 that connects to external services to execute tasks for users, expanding to the United Kingdom in November 2026. Under a November 24, 2026 Walmart integration, Muse relays shopping lists to the Walmart Agent Gateway, while Walmart handles product matching, cart building, pricing, and user-approved checkout.

Meta Muse is a personal artificial intelligence agent introduced by Meta in September 2026 to connect with external services and execute multi-step tasks such as shopping on a user's behalf. Following its U.S. rollout, Meta expanded Muse to the United Kingdom on November 23, 2026. ## Meta Muse launch and UK expansion

Meta introduced Muse on September 8, 2026, as a personal AI agent designed to carry out everyday tasks across external services rather than only answering text prompts.[1] On November 23, 2026, Meta announced Muse's expansion to the United Kingdom as a user-controlled personal AI agent.[2] On November 24, 2026, U.S. retailers Walmart and Gopuff announced shopping integrations with Muse ahead of Black Friday and the holiday shopping season.[3][4][6]

## How Meta and Walmart divide search, cart, and checkout

On November 24, 2026, Walmart announced that customers can shop Walmart's Black Friday deals directly through Meta Muse via the Walmart Agent Gateway, with Gopuff also joining as a retail partner.[3][4][6] Walmart and TechCrunch outlined a clear split of responsibilities between Meta's agent and Walmart's commerce backend:[3][4]

| Shopping Step | Handled by Meta Muse | Handled by Walmart (Agent Gateway) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Request intake | User opens Muse app, signs into Walmart account, submits shopping list or item prompt | Receives request through Walmart Agent Gateway |
| Product search & pricing | Relays user intent and presents options | Searches catalog, matches products, applies Everyday Low Prices, Black Friday deals, and membership benefits (e.g., free delivery) |
| Cart & checkout | Coordinates basket confirmation with user | Builds shopping cart and processes checkout under user rules |
| Account privacy | Does not access customer's Walmart shopping history or other personal Walmart data | Enforces user-set spending limits, pre-purchase approval requirements, and order review |

TechCrunch noted that Walmart's open gateway approach contrasts with Amazon's steps to block third-party AI agents from purchasing on customers' behalf.[4]

## User permissions and spending guardrails

Through the Walmart Agent Gateway, customers decide what actions Muse is permitted to take.[3] According to TechCrunch's summary of Walmart's safeguards, Muse is blocked from reading a shopper's past Walmart order history or unrelated personal account information, and users can configure spending caps, require explicit approval before any purchase is finalized, and review orders placed through the agent.[4][5]

## Meta Muse unverified metrics as of November 2026

As of November 27, 2026, neither Meta nor Walmart has disclosed actual transaction volumes, conversion rates, or active user counts for the Muse-Walmart shopping integration.[3][4]

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Meta Muse?

Meta Muse is a personal AI agent launched by Meta in September 2026 that connects to external services and retail gateways to perform tasks such as product discovery and shopping on behalf of users. [1][3]

### In the Walmart integration, what does Meta handle versus what does Walmart handle?

After a user signs into their Walmart account via Muse, Muse takes the user's shopping list or prompt and passes it to the Walmart Agent Gateway. Walmart handles catalog search, product matching, applying Everyday Low Prices, Black Friday deals, and membership perks, building the cart, and completing checkout under user-set spending and approval rules. [3][4]

### Can Meta Muse see a shopper's past Walmart purchase history?

No. According to Walmart and TechCrunch, Muse does not receive access to a customer's Walmart shopping history or other personal Walmart account data. [4]

### Where is Meta Muse available as of November 2026?

Following its September 2026 U.S. launch, Meta announced Muse's expansion to the United Kingdom on November 23, 2026. [1][2]
