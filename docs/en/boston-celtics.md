## Boston Celtics overview and 2026-27 NBA Cup context

The Boston Celtics are a Boston-based NBA franchise competing in the Eastern Conference and assigned to East Group C for the Emirates NBA Cup 2026.[2][3][4] Entering their 2026-11-24 road game in Washington, Boston was seeking to end a four-game losing streak during a stretch of three games in four nights.[1][5]

Following the 131-116 victory over Washington on 2026-11-24, the Celtics improved to 3-0 in East Group C play, mathematically eliminating the Wizards from tournament contention.[3][5] Final group standings are not yet decided as of 2026-11-25; CBS Sports reported that Boston faces Atlanta on Friday night with the winner finishing atop East Group C.[5]

## Boston Celtics 131-116 win over Washington Wizards (2026-11-24)

The Boston Celtics beat the Washington Wizards 131-116 on Tuesday, 2026-11-24 at Capital One Arena in Washington, D.C.[2][4] Boston led 34-28 after the first quarter and erupted for a 46-21 second quarter with 10 three-pointers to take an 80-49 halftime lead, reaching a peak advantage of 34 points.[1][4][5][6]

Washington outscored Boston 40-24 in the third quarter and cut the deficit to five points with 3:19 remaining in the fourth quarter.[4][5][6] Baylor Scheierman, scoreless for the first 45-and-a-half minutes, then scored eight consecutive Celtics points in 1 minute and 15 seconds (a three-pointer with 2:31 left, two free throws, and a 31-foot three-pointer for a 127-114 lead) to secure the win.[1]
