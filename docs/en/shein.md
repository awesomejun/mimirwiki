# SHEIN

> SHEIN is a Singapore-based online fast-fashion retailer that ships roughly one million packages per day to the United States. On November 24, 2026, the U.S. Federal Trade Commission filed a complaint and proposed $100 million consumer-redress settlement alleging deceptive shipping promises, customer service barriers, suppression of negative reviews, and misleading supply-chain claims.

SHEIN is a Singapore-headquartered online fast-fashion retail platform founded in China in 2008 that ships nearly one million packages daily to customers in the United States. On November 24, 2026, the U.S. Federal Trade Commission (FTC) filed a federal court complaint and a proposed stipulated order requiring SHEIN to pay $100 million in consumer redress to resolve allegations of deceptive and unfair practices. The FTC alleged that SHEIN failed to meet shipping promises, maintained inadequate customer service, suppressed negative product reviews, and made unsupported supply-chain and cotton-origin claims. Earlier in 2026, Texas Attorney General Ken Paxton also filed a state lawsuit against Shein US Services, LLC alleging violations of the Texas Deceptive Trade Practices Act.

**Also known as:** Shein, Shein US Services, LLC

## SHEIN business profile and U.S. market presence

SHEIN is an online fast-fashion retailer founded in China in 2008 and headquartered in Singapore.[4][7] According to DLA Piper's summary of state court filings, SHEIN launched its U.S. marketplace in 2022 and reported $32 billion in U.S. revenue in 2023.[7] In its November 2026 federal complaint, the U.S. Federal Trade Commission (FTC) stated that SHEIN accounts for roughly half of fast-fashion sales in the United States and ships nearly one million packages to U.S. consumers each day.[1][5]

CBS News reported that SHEIN went public on the Hong Kong stock exchange in September 2026.[4]

## SHEIN November 2026 FTC complaint and alleged practices

On November 24, 2026, the FTC filed a complaint against three SHEIN corporate entities in the U.S. District Court for the Central District of California alleging violations of Section 5 of the FTC Act, the Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule, and the Rule on the Use of Consumer Reviews and Testimonials.[1][2][6] According to the FTC, SHEIN marketed fast, transparent, and reliable shipping, yet tens of thousands of consumers reported orders arriving days, weeks, or months late, or not arriving at all, without legally required delay notices.[1]

The FTC complaint also alleged that despite shipping nearly one million packages daily to the United States, SHEIN employed only five customer service agents as of September 2025 to handle telephone inquiries from American consumers and did not list a phone number for those agents on its website.[1][4] Regarding customer reviews, the FTC alleged that SHEIN systematically published positive product reviews while suppressing or deleting critical reviews, including internal guidance to exclude extremely negative comments.[1][5] The agency further accused SHEIN of making unsupported claims about its evoluSHEIN products and supplier social-compliance audits, as well as deceptive claims about cotton sourcing and origin.[1][4][6]

## SHEIN proposed $100 million FTC order and company response

Alongside its November 24, 2026 complaint, the FTC filed a proposed stipulated order requiring SHEIN to pay $100 million to be used for consumer redress.[1][4] Under the proposed order, SHEIN would be barred from making misleading claims about shipping and delivery times and from suppressing negative product reviews, and would be required to provide adequate customer service.[4]

A SHEIN spokesperson told CBS News and Reuters that the agreement does not constitute an admission of liability and that the company continues to disagree with aspects of the FTC's characterization of its business and compliance practices.[4][5] As of November 26, 2026, final entry of the federal court order and the specific distribution procedure for consumer redress payments have not yet been announced.[1][6]

## SHEIN 2026 Texas attorney general lawsuit

Prior to the federal FTC action, Texas Attorney General Ken Paxton filed a civil lawsuit on February 20, 2026, against Shein US Services, LLC alleging false, deceptive, and misleading practices under the Texas Deceptive Trade Practices Act.[3][7] According to DLA Piper's analysis of the petition, the Texas complaint targets product safety concerns and alleged misrepresentations regarding consumer data practices, and followed Texas Governor Greg Abbott's addition of SHEIN to the state's Prohibited Technologies List for state employees and devices after a Texas Cyber Command threat assessment.[7]

## Frequently asked questions

### Why did the U.S. Federal Trade Commission sue SHEIN in November 2026?

On November 24, 2026, the FTC alleged that SHEIN violated federal consumer protection rules by failing to keep shipping promises and provide required delay notices, maintaining inadequate customer service (five phone agents for nearly one million daily U.S. packages as of September 2025), suppressing negative product reviews, and making deceptive cotton-origin and supplier audit claims. [1]

### What are the main terms of SHEIN's proposed $100 million FTC settlement?

The proposed stipulated order filed in the U.S. District Court for the Central District of California requires SHEIN to pay $100 million for consumer redress, stop misrepresenting shipping times, stop suppressing negative reviews, and provide adequate customer service. [1][4][6]

### Has the consumer redress claim process for the $100 million SHEIN settlement opened?

As of November 26, 2026, the FTC has filed the proposed settlement order with the federal court, and neither the court's final order nor a consumer refund claim procedure has been announced. [1][6]

### Did SHEIN admit wrongdoing in the FTC settlement?

No. A SHEIN spokesperson stated that the settlement agreement does not constitute an admission of liability and that the company continues to disagree with aspects of the FTC's characterization of its business and compliance practices. [4][5]
