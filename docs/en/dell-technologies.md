## Dell Technologies corporate overview

Dell Technologies Inc. (NYSE: DELL) is headquartered at One Dell Way in Round Rock, Texas.[1][3] The company operates two main operating divisions: the Infrastructure Solutions Group (ISG), which sells servers, storage, and networking equipment to enterprises and data centers, and the Client Solutions Group (CSG), which sells PCs and laptops.[2][5][6]

## Dell Technologies Q3 fiscal 2027 financial results

On November 24, 2026, Dell Technologies reported record results for its fiscal third quarter 2027 (ended October 30, 2026) in a Form 8-K and Exhibit 99.1 filed with the SEC.[1][2][3] Quarterly revenue more than doubled year over year (+103%) to $54.9 billion, GAAP diluted EPS rose 287% to $8.82, and non-GAAP diluted EPS increased 265% to $9.45.[2][3]

Operating cash flow reached a Q3 record of $4.5 billion, non-GAAP operating expenses fell to 7% of revenue (the lowest ratio in the company's 42-year history), and Dell returned a record $4.9 billion to shareholders during the quarter while adding $25 billion to its share repurchase authorization.[2][5]

## Dell Technologies AI server surge and segment performance

Nearly all of Dell's Q3 FY27 top-line expansion came from Infrastructure Solutions Group (ISG), where revenue surged 198% year over year to $42.0 billion ($41.97 billion), including $22.0 billion in AI server sales (+291% YoY).[5][6] Vice Chairman and COO Jeff Clarke reported $60.2 billion in new Q3 AI orders and a record quarter-end AI backlog of $132.3 billion, noting that orders are vetted for customer quality, capital, land, and power and that more than 7,500 customers are deploying Dell AI Factories.[2][3]

In contrast, the Client Solutions Group (PCs and laptops) grew 3% year over year to $12.9 billion, with financial coverage noting that rising memory prices weighed on PC segment revenue.[5][6]

## Dell Technologies fiscal 2027 outlook and open uncertainties

Dell raised its full-year fiscal 2027 guidance for the third time, projecting revenue of $202.0 billion (+78% YoY), AI server revenue of $78 billion, GAAP diluted EPS of $28.24 (+225% YoY), and non-GAAP diluted EPS of $29.68 (+188% YoY).[2][3][5]

Whether Dell achieves its full-year FY27 revenue and earnings targets, as well as the exact multi-quarter delivery schedule for converting its $132.3 billion AI backlog into recognized revenue, remains unverified as of November 26, 2026.[2][5]
