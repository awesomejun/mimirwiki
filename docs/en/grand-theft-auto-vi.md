# Grand Theft Auto VI

> Released worldwide on November 19, 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, Grand Theft Auto VI generated $3.79 billion in day-one retail sales and 32.5 million pre-release units sold, shattering global box-office records across all entertainment media. Priced at $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition, the game debuted with a 99 Metacritic score following an extensive marketing campaign that included a June 2026 pre-order opening and a nearly 30-minute Extended Look gameplay special on Netflix.

Grand Theft Auto VI (commonly abbreviated as GTA VI or GTA 6) is an open-world action-adventure video game developed and published by Rockstar Games, a wholly owned publishing label of Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc. (NASDAQ: TTWO). Set in the fictional U.S. state of Leonida - centered on a contemporary recreation of Vice City - the single-player story follows dual protagonists Jason and Lucia as they navigate a state-wide criminal conspiracy after a botched heist.

## Overview, Setting, and Narrative Premise

**Grand Theft Auto VI** is the eighth main entry and sixteenth overall installment in the *Grand Theft Auto* franchise created by **Rockstar Games**, a subsidiary of **Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc.** (NASDAQ: `TTWO`, SEC CIK `0000946581`) [1][2]. Succeeding *Grand Theft Auto V* (2013), which had sold-in nearly 235 million units prior to *GTA VI*'s launch and helped push cumulative franchise sales to nearly 480 million units, *Grand Theft Auto VI* was released globally on **November 19, 2026** [2][3].

The game takes place in the fictional U.S. state of **Leonida**, a satirical open-world rendition of Florida anchored by a modern-day **Vice City** [2][5]. The single-player narrative centers on two playable criminal partners, **Jason** and **Lucia** [2][6]. According to Rockstar Games' official synopsis, after an seemingly easy score goes wrong, Jason and Lucia are drawn into the darkest side of America's sunniest state, caught in the middle of a criminal conspiracy stretching across Leonida and forced to rely on each other to survive [2][5].

## Pre-Release Timeline, Netflix Showcase, and Launch Rollout

Following its December 2023 debut trailer (Trailer 1), Rockstar Games and Take-Two Interactive executed a multi-stage promotional campaign leading to the November 19, 2026 launch during Take-Two's fiscal year 2027 (FY2027, ending March 31, 2027) [3][5]: - **June 2026 Pre-Order Launch**: Digital and physical retail pre-orders opened in June 2026, generating initial consumer demand that Take-Two reported in its Q2 FY27 Form 10-Q as outpacing all historical benchmarks for the *Grand Theft Auto* series [3]. - **Netflix 'An Extended Look' Showcase**: Prior to launch, Rockstar Games debuted **'An Extended Look'**, a nearly 30-minute gameplay special distributed on **Netflix** and Rockstar's official channels, alongside Trailer 2 and a Launch Trailer [3][5]. - **November 2026 Rollout**: Rockstar opened the official Launch Gallery of Leonida screenshots on **November 5, 2026**, enabled console digital pre-loading on **November 10, 2026**, and unlocked the game worldwide on **November 19, 2026** across the PlayStation Store, Microsoft Store, Rockstar Games Store, and physical retailers [2][5].

## Platforms, Pricing Editions, and Technical Specifications

At launch on November 19, 2026, *Grand Theft Auto VI* shipped on **PlayStation 5** (where Rockstar's official store page highlights *'Plays Best On PlayStation 5'*) and **Xbox Series X|S** (including Cloud Gaming on Xbox consoles) [5][6]. On the Microsoft Xbox Store and PlayStation Store, Rockstar distributed the title across two main editions and two digital add-ons [5][6][7]:

| Edition / Add-On | US Retail Price | Included Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Grand Theft Auto VI (Standard Edition)** | **$79.99** | Base single-player game set in Vice City and Leonida [6][7]. |
| **Grand Theft Auto VI: Ultimate Edition** | **$99.99** | Base game plus the Ultimate Edition Upgrade, featuring an exclusive collection of items threaded across Jason and Lucia's story [5][6][7]. |
| **Ultimate Edition Upgrade** | **$20.00** | Standalone upgrade add-on for Standard Edition owners [6]. |
| **Vintage Vice City Pack** | **$4.99** | Digital add-on providing nostalgic neon-era Vice City cosmetic benefits [5][6]. |

Console members subscribed to Rockstar's **GTA+** membership program also receive rotating optional in-game benefits for Jason and Lucia [3][5]. Technically, the console builds support hardware **Ray Tracing**, **HDR10**, **Dolby Atmos**, **DTS:X**, spatial audio, 13 accessibility features, and 13 supported languages, and carry an **ESRB Mature 17+ (M)** rating [2][5][6].

## Record-Breaking $3.79 Billion Day-One Sales and Critical Reception

On **November 20, 2026**, Take-Two Interactive filed a Current Report on **Form 8-K** (Item 7.01, Exhibit 99.1) with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission announcing that *Grand Theft Auto VI* achieved **$3.79 billion in day-one worldwide retail sales** (based on company estimates through the end of November 19, 2026) [1][2][4]. The launch shattered all prior entertainment box-office records across film, music, and video games, making *Grand Theft Auto VI* the highest-grossing entertainment launch in history [2][4].

Take-Two and Rockstar Games also disclosed that *Grand Theft Auto VI* sold **32.5 million units pre-release**, establishing a new all-time pre-release sales record across all gaming platforms, and debuted with a near-perfect aggregate score of **99 on Metacritic**, making it the highest-rated game of all time at launch [2][4]. Rockstar Games President and Founder **Sam Houser** stated in the filing that the studio was 'incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world' as Rockstar entered a new chapter in its history [2].

## Frequently asked questions

### When was Grand Theft Auto VI released and on which platforms?

Grand Theft Auto VI was released worldwide on November 19, 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S (with digital pre-loading beginning on November 10, 2026). [2][3][5]

### How much money and how many copies did Grand Theft Auto VI sell on day one?

According to Take-Two Interactive's Form 8-K filed with the SEC on November 20, 2026, Grand Theft Auto VI generated $3.79 billion in day-one global retail sales through the end of November 19, 2026 and sold 32.5 million units pre-release, setting the all-time launch record across any entertainment medium. [1][2][4]

### What is the Metacritic score of Grand Theft Auto VI?

At launch in November 2026, Grand Theft Auto VI achieved a score of 99 out of 100 on Metacritic, which Rockstar Games and Take-Two Interactive cited as the highest-rated game release of all time. [2][4]

### How much do the Standard Edition and Ultimate Edition of GTA VI cost?

In the United States, the Grand Theft Auto VI Standard Edition is priced at $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition is priced at $99.99. Players can also purchase the standalone Ultimate Edition Upgrade for $20.00 and the Vintage Vice City Pack digital add-on for $4.99. [5][6][7]

### Where is Grand Theft Auto VI set and who are the main characters?

Grand Theft Auto VI is set in Vice City and across the fictional U.S. state of Leonida (based on Florida). The single-player campaign follows dual protagonists Jason and Lucia as they confront a state-wide criminal conspiracy after a score goes wrong. [2][5][6]
