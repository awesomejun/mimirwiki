## FC Barcelona club overview and identity

FC Barcelona is a professional football club based in Barcelona, Spain, whose institutional identity centers on five core values: respect, effort, ambition, teamwork, and humility.[4] The club operates youth and community programs including La Masia, Barça Academy, and the Barça Foundation.[4]

In its modern sporting history, FC Barcelona won three UEFA Champions League titles during the 2008-20 period and appointed Hansi Flick as manager during the club's 125th anniversary year.[5]

## FC Barcelona 4-1 victory over Sabah FK in Baku

FC Barcelona defeated Sabah FK 4-1 on Wednesday, November 25, 2026, at Baku Olympic Stadium in Baku, Azerbaijan, in Matchday 5 of the 2026-27 UEFA Champions League league phase.[1][6] Official club match records listed attendance at 66,321 with Nick Walsh serving as referee.[1]

The fixture was the first-ever meeting between FC Barcelona and Sabah FK and represented the longest European away trip in FC Barcelona's history.[3][7] Lamine Yamal missed the trip to Baku because of minor discomfort in his right ankle.[7]

## FC Barcelona goalscorers against Sabah FK

FC Barcelona built a 4-0 lead by the hour mark behind a brace from Anthony Gordon and rapid second-half goals from Karim Adeyemi and Gabriel Jesus.[6][7] Gordon opened the scoring in the 31st minute and scored his second goal in the 51st minute.[7]

Karim Adeyemi, starting on the right wing in place of Lamine Yamal, added the third goal in the 55th minute, and Gabriel Jesus made it 4-0 in the 60th minute.[6][7] Joy Lance Mickels scored Sabah's lone goal later in the second half.[7]

## FC Barcelona in the 2026-27 UEFA Champions League league phase

FC Barcelona's win in Baku gave the club four victories from its first five matches in the eight-fixture UEFA Champions League league phase.[2][6] Reuters reporting via Arab News noted that the result left FC Barcelona fourth in the league-phase standings while Sabah sat 32nd, strengthening Barcelona's push for a top-eight finish.[6]

UEFA confirmed the eight-match league-phase schedule in August 2026, leaving three remaining league-phase fixtures for FC Barcelona after Matchday 5.[2]
