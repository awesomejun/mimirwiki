## Aaron Rodgers career overview

Aaron Rodgers is an NFL quarterback who played college football at California and is in his 22nd NFL season as of 2026-11-25.[2][5] Rodgers is a four-time NFL MVP and won a Super Bowl with the Green Bay Packers following the 2010 season under head coach Mike McCarthy.[5]

For the 2026 season, which Rodgers has stated is his final NFL season, he joined the Pittsburgh Steelers to reunite with head coach Mike McCarthy, who replaced Mike Tomlin.[5] On the Steelers' roster, Rodgers wears jersey number 8 and is listed at 6 feet 2 inches and 223 pounds.[2]

## Aaron Rodgers November 2026 benching by the Steelers

Aaron Rodgers was ruled inactive for the Pittsburgh Steelers' Friday home game against the Denver Broncos in a statement issued at 11:10 p.m. ET on Tuesday, 2026-11-24, by Steelers spokesman and director of communications Burt Lauten.[1][3][4] The statement named Mason Rudolph as the starting quarterback against Denver and said Rodgers would be inactive for Friday's game.[1]

NFL.com reported that Rodgers was not listed on Pittsburgh's Tuesday injury report, and both NFL Network and ESPN reported the move was a coaching decision rather than an injury or illness.[3][4][6] The Guardian cited ESPN reporting that the Steelers gave Rodgers permission to miss Wednesday practices following the decision.[6] Whether Rodgers will return to the starting lineup later in the 2026 season has not been announced as of 2026-11-25.[1][4]
