# NVIDIA

> In November 2026, NVIDIA underwent a rapid leadership transition alongside record financial results: co-founder Jensen Huang resigned as President, CEO, and director effective November 11, 2026 (with EVP and CFO Colette M. Kress serving as Interim CEO and Harvey C. Jones named Chair of the Board), before the Board voted unanimously on November 22, 2026 (announced November 23) to reinstate Huang as President, CEO, and director effective immediately while Jones remains Chair and Kress continues as EVP and CFO. Alongside forming a National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee and beginning a Chief Compliance Officer search, NVIDIA reported Q3 FY2027 revenue of $115.8 billion (+20% QoQ, +103% YoY; Data Center $107.6 billion), GAAP net income of $65.3 billion ($2.70 EPS), initial Vera Rubin NVL72 shipments, and an agreement to acquire Hugging Face.

NVIDIA Corporation (NASDAQ: NVDA) is an American multinational technology and fabless semiconductor company headquartered in Santa Clara, California. Founded in April 1993 by Jensen Huang, Chris Malachowsky, and Curtis Priem, NVIDIA designs graphics processing units (GPUs), ARM-based data center CPUs, high-speed interconnects (NVLink, Quantum InfiniBand, and Spectrum-X Ethernet), and the CUDA accelerated computing software stack powering global artificial intelligence infrastructure.

## Corporate Overview and Business Segments

NVIDIA Corporation was founded in April 1993 by Jensen Huang, Chris Malachowsky, and Curtis Priem to pioneer 3D graphics acceleration, inventing the graphics processing unit (GPU) in 1999 and launching the **CUDA** parallel programming model in 2006 [1][11]. Headquartered in Santa Clara, California (SEC CIK `0001045810`), NVIDIA operates a fabless manufacturing model, relying on industry partners for wafer fabrication, high-bandwidth memory, and advanced packaging [1].

In fiscal year 2027, NVIDIA reports its financial results across two reportable operating segments (**Compute & Networking**, which generated $106.0 billion in Q3 FY27, and **Graphics**, which generated $9.8 billion in Q3 FY27) and two market platforms, Data Center and Edge Computing [1][4][5]: 1. **Data Center**: Encompasses accelerated AI computing platforms (Hopper, Blackwell, Blackwell Ultra, and Vera Rubin rack-scale NVL72 systems), ARM-based Grace and Vera data center CPUs, high-speed networking (NVLink, Quantum InfiniBand, Spectrum-X Ethernet), and NVIDIA AI Enterprise software across Hyperscale ($60.1 billion in Q3 FY27) and AI Clouds, Industrial, & Enterprise ($47.5 billion in Q3 FY27) customer groups [4][5][8]. 2. **Edge Computing** ($8.2 billion in Q3 FY27): Includes GeForce RTX GPUs, DLSS 5, RTX Spark laptops, the DGX Spark 64GB desktop AI system, autonomous-driving development tools, and the Isaac robotics platform [4][5].

## November 2026 CEO Resignation, Reinstatement, and Governance Reforms

In November 2026, NVIDIA underwent a major series of leadership and corporate governance changes [2][6][7][9][10]. In a Form 8-K (Item 5.02) filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on November 12, 2026 and an accompanying corporate announcement, NVIDIA disclosed that on **November 11, 2026**, the Board of Directors accepted the resignation of co-founder **Jensen (Jen-Hsun) Huang** as President, Chief Executive Officer, and a member of the Board of Directors, effective immediately [2][6]. Simultaneously, the board took two structural leadership steps [2][6]: - Appointed Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer **Colette M. Kress** (age 59, who has served as NVIDIA's EVP and CFO since September 2013) as **Interim Chief Executive Officer** while continuing in her role as EVP and CFO during the board's executive search [2][6]. - Appointed longtime director **Harvey C. Jones** (age 73, an NVIDIA board member since 1993, Managing Partner of Square Wave Ventures, and former CEO and Executive Chairman of Synopsys, Inc.) as NVIDIA's first-ever **Chair of the Board** [2][6].

On **November 17, 2026**, NVIDIA's Board of Directors announced the establishment of the **National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee**, a standing board committee comprised of independent directors created to oversee the company's national security policies, export control compliance, and global trade risk management [7][9]. The committee is chaired by director **Suzanne Nora Johnson** alongside directors **Stephen C. Neal** and **John O. Dabiri**, with NVIDIA's National Security Team and Global Trade Compliance Team reporting directly to the committee and, on day-to-day operations, to Interim CEO Colette Kress, as stated in the November 17 announcement [7].

On **November 22, 2026** (announced publicly via NVIDIA Newsroom and an SEC Form 8-K Item 5.02 filing on **November 23, 2026**), NVIDIA's Board of Directors voted unanimously to **reinstate Jensen Huang as President, Chief Executive Officer, and a member of the Board of Directors**, effective immediately [9][10]. According to NVIDIA's November 23 announcement and Form 8-K filing [9][10]: - The board stated that it voted unanimously to reinstate Huang following a series of discussions and meetings with him, after which the board was satisfied with respect to his candor and ability to lead NVIDIA, and that based on input received from senior levels of the United States government, the board is confident that Huang's reinstatement poses no national security risk [9]. - **Harvey C. Jones** remains **Chair of the Board**, and following Huang's reinstatement, **Colette M. Kress** concluded her service as Interim CEO and continues to serve as NVIDIA's **Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer** [9][10]. - NVIDIA stated that, as previously announced in light of reporting that Huang had been the target of a foreign intelligence effort, the board formed a committee comprised solely of independent directors (assisted by independent counsel) to investigate, and the board reiterated that the independent committee's investigation will proceed to conclusion with Huang's full support and cooperation [9]. - In addition to the National Security and Export Control Committee engaging specialized national security counsel and advisors, NVIDIA announced that it is commencing a search to fill a newly created **Chief Compliance Officer** position to oversee compliance across the company and report to the Nominating and Corporate Governance Committee, and will continue to cooperate with ongoing government investigations and inquiries [9].

## Q3 FY2027 Financial Results and Supply Commitments

On November 17, 2026, NVIDIA filed a Form 8-K (Item 2.02) along with Exhibit 99.1 (Q3 FY27 earnings release) and Exhibit 99.2 (CFO Commentary) reporting record financial results for the third quarter of fiscal 2027 (ended October 25, 2026) [3][4][5]:

NVIDIA reported that its supply commitments rose to **$390 billion** at the close of Q3 FY27 (from $279 billion a quarter earlier), primarily related to multi-year procurement of memory supply, alongside $102 billion in cloud service agreements and data center lease commitments (up from $74 billion) [5]. During Q3 FY27, NVIDIA returned **$28.1 billion** to shareholders via share repurchases and cash dividends after the board approved a $150.0 billion increase to the share repurchase authorization in September 2026 ($227.3 billion remaining) [4][5]. For the fourth quarter of fiscal 2027, NVIDIA guided total revenue to **$130.0 billion, plus or minus 3%** (assuming no Data Center compute revenue from China), with GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins expected to be between **71.0% and 72.0%** [4][5].

## Vera Rubin Architecture, Hugging Face Acquisition, and Product Ecosystem

During Q3 FY2027, NVIDIA ramped production and began shipping its **Vera Rubin NVL72** data center platform, alongside continued growth of Blackwell Ultra [4][5][8]. The Vera Rubin generation pairs the **Rubin GPU** and custom **Vera CPU** inside the rack-scale **Vera Rubin NVL72** system, complemented by Spectrum-X Ethernet and Quantum InfiniBand networking fabrics [4][5][8].

In its Q3 FY27 financial results release and CFO commentary on November 17, 2026, NVIDIA announced an agreement to acquire **Hugging Face**; the CFO commentary describes the acquisition as pending [4][5]. In **Edge Computing**, NVIDIA launched **DLSS 5** for GeForce RTX 50 Series GPUs, began shipping **RTX Spark** laptops through PC makers, and added a **DGX Spark 64GB** desktop model [4].
