# Martha Firestone Ford

> Martha Firestone Ford owned the Detroit Lions for more than 60 years after she and her husband William Clay Ford bought the team in 1963. She led the franchise as principal owner from 2014 until June 2020, when daughter Sheila Ford Hamp succeeded her, and died on 2026-11-24 at age 101.

Martha Firestone Ford (1925-2026) was the longtime owner and chair emeritus of the National Football League's Detroit Lions, serving as principal owner and chairwoman from 2014 to 2020 before passing leadership to her daughter, Sheila Ford Hamp. The Detroit Lions announced on 2026-11-24 that Martha Firestone Ford passed away at age 101 at her home surrounded by her family.

## Martha Firestone Ford early life and family background

Martha Firestone Ford was born in Akron, Ohio, and was the granddaughter of American industrialist Harvey S. Firestone, founder of the Firestone Tire and Rubber Company.[1][5] She graduated from Vassar College in 1946 and married William Clay Ford Sr., a grandson of Henry Ford, in 1947.[1][5]

Together, Martha Firestone Ford and William Clay Ford Sr. had four children who later took leadership roles within the Detroit Lions organization: Sheila Ford Hamp, Martha Ford Morse, William Clay Ford Jr., and Elizabeth Ford Kontulis.[1]

## Martha Firestone Ford leadership of the Detroit Lions

Martha Firestone Ford was an owner of the Detroit Lions for more than 60 years after she and her husband purchased full control of the franchise in November 1963.[1][6] Following William Clay Ford's passing in 2014, she assumed sole control of the franchise as principal owner and chairwoman at age 88.[1][5]

Known for a hands-on style, Martha Firestone Ford regularly attended NFL owners' meetings, engaged with fans at team events, and replaced franchise leadership in pursuit of winning results.[3][5] In her first season as principal owner in 2014, the Detroit Lions finished 11-5 and reached the NFC playoffs.[3]

## Martha Firestone Ford 2020 transition to Sheila Ford Hamp

Martha Firestone Ford stepped aside from principal leadership of the Detroit Lions on June 23, 2020, transitioning to the role of Owner/Chair Emeritus.[1][3][4] Her daughter, Sheila Ford Hamp, succeeded her as Principal Owner and Chair, while her other three children continued as Owners and Vice Chairs.[1][4]

Prior to taking over, Sheila Ford Hamp had attended team functions and NFL meetings alongside her mother and served on the NFL's Super Bowl and Major Events advisory committee.[3][4] Martha Firestone Ford continued serving in her emeritus capacity until her death.[1]

## Martha Firestone Ford passing and Detroit Lions tributes

Martha Firestone Ford died on November 24, 2026, at age 101 at her home surrounded by family, according to the Detroit Lions' official announcement.[1][5] Media reports noted that no cause of death was released, and funeral details were not included in the team's announcements.[1][6]

On November 25, 2026, the Detroit Lions announced tributes including a moment of silence at Ford Field before Thursday's kickoff against the Chicago Bears, customized 'MFF' helmet decals for players, and a jersey patch scheduled to debut on Sunday, December 6, against the Atlanta Falcons.[2] Lions President and CEO Rich Haddad and former President and CEO Rod Wood issued statements honoring her stewardship and commitment to Detroit.[2]
