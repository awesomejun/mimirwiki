## Nintendo Switch 2 Black Friday 2026 U.S. promotions

On November 12, 2026, Nintendo announced its 2026 U.S. Black Friday holiday promotions, led by a $50 discount on the *Nintendo Switch 2 + Mario Kart World Bundle* to $499.99 MSRP from November 22 through December 5, 2026, while supplies last.[1] Nintendo stated that the bundle offers a combined value of $87.98 compared with purchasing the system, game, and membership separately.[4]

## Nintendo Switch 2 sales in Nintendo's November 2026 results

In its six-month financial results presentation dated November 6, 2026 (following the November 5, 2026 financial release), Nintendo reported that Nintendo Switch 2 hardware sales totaled 6.49 million units, down 37.4% year on year, while Nintendo Switch 2 software sales rose 18.8% year on year to 24.49 million units.[2][3] During the same six-month period, original Nintendo Switch hardware sales declined 32.7% to 1.27 million units while original Nintendo Switch software sales increased 6.8% to 65.73 million units.[2]

Across the company, Nintendo reported ordinary profit rising 27.4% year on year to 300.6 billion yen and profit attributable to owners of parent rising 11.0% to 220.9 billion yen, leaving its consolidated financial and hardware/software unit forecasts for the fiscal year ending March 2027 unchanged from May 8, 2026.[2]
