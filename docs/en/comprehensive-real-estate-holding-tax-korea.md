# Comprehensive Real Estate Holding Tax (South Korea)

> South Korea's Comprehensive Real Estate Holding Tax applies to housing exceeding KRW 900 million in assessed value (KRW 1.2 billion for one-household one-home owners) and land above statutory thresholds as of June 1. For 2026, the NTS notified about 820,000 taxpayers (KRW 6.0 trillion total), with payment and installment applications due 2026-12-15 and deferral applications due 2026-12-12.

The Comprehensive Real Estate Holding Tax (종합부동산세) is a South Korean national tax administered by the National Tax Service (NTS) and levied on individuals and entities whose aggregate officially assessed values of housing or land as of June 1 exceed statutory deduction thresholds. On 2026-11-26, the NTS announced that 2026 tax notices were dispatched sequentially starting 2026-11-24 to about 820,000 taxpayers (excluding overlap) totaling KRW 6.0 trillion, with payment due by 2026-12-15.

**Also known as:** 종합부동산세, 종부세, Korea Comprehensive Real Estate Tax

## Comprehensive Real Estate Holding Tax overview and statutory thresholds

The Comprehensive Real Estate Holding Tax is a national property holding tax in South Korea levied on owners whose aggregate officially assessed property values as of June 1 exceed statutory deduction amounts.[2][5] Under current rules reported alongside the 2026 notice, the deduction threshold is KRW 900 million for housing (KRW 1.2 billion for eligible one-household one-home owners), KRW 500 million for comprehensive aggregate land, and KRW 8 billion for separate aggregate land.[5]

## Comprehensive Real Estate Holding Tax 2026 assessment figures

Comprehensive Real Estate Holding Tax notices for the 2026 tax year were dispatched sequentially by the National Tax Service starting November 24, 2026, covering approximately 820,000 taxpayers (excluding 20,000 overlapping housing-and-land taxpayers) and KRW 6.0 trillion in total notified tax.[1][2] By category, housing notices accounted for about 730,000 taxpayers (exact figure 726,000) and KRW 2.3 trillion, while land notices accounted for 110,000 taxpayers and KRW 3.7 trillion.[2][4]

Korea Economic Daily and KBC News reported that total notified taxpayers (about 817,000) rose roughly 30 percent from about 629,000 in 2025, with total tax up KRW 700 billion and average tax per taxpayer reaching KRW 1,994,000 (+24.1 percent).[4][6] The increase was driven by rising officially assessed apartment prices (up 9.1 percent nationwide and 18.6 percent in Seoul), with Seoul accounting for 459,000 housing taxpayers (63.2 percent) and the Seoul metropolitan area accounting for 86.3 percent.[4][6]

## Comprehensive Real Estate Holding Tax payment, installment, and deferral rules

Comprehensive Real Estate Holding Tax notified for 2026 must be paid by December 15, 2026.[1][2] Taxpayers whose payable amount (including special rural development tax) exceeds KRW 3 million may apply via Hometax by December 15 to pay in installments over up to six months (through June 15, 2027) without interest surcharge.[2]

Eligible one-household one-home taxpayers who are seniors aged 60 or older or long-term holders of five years or more may apply by December 12, 2026, to defer housing tax payment until transfer, gift, or inheritance if they provide tax collateral; the NTS sent separate notices to 28,000 eligible taxpayers.[2] Taxpayers whose notice differs from actual ownership facts or who missed exclusion or special-treatment filings may submit a voluntary self-filing via Hometax by December 15, which automatically cancels the initial notice.[3][5] Final actual collection totals for 2026 remain unverified until after the filing and payment period closes.[2]

## Comprehensive Real Estate Holding Tax 2026 regional concentration

Comprehensive Real Estate Holding Tax housing notices in 2026 were heavily concentrated in the Seoul metropolitan area following an 18.6 percent rise in officially assessed apartment prices in Seoul and a 6.4 percent rise in Gyeonggi Province.[4] According to KBC News citing Ministry of Economy and Finance figures, Seoul accounted for 459,000 housing taxpayers (63.2 percent, up 40.1 percent year over year), and Seoul, Gyeonggi, and Incheon combined represented 86.3 percent of all housing taxpayers.[4]

## Frequently asked questions

### Who receives a 2026 Comprehensive Real Estate Holding Tax notice in South Korea?

Property owners whose aggregate officially assessed values as of June 1, 2026, exceed KRW 900 million for housing (KRW 1.2 billion for one-household one-home owners), KRW 500 million for comprehensive aggregate land, or KRW 8 billion for separate aggregate land receive notices. [5]

### When is the 2026 Comprehensive Real Estate Holding Tax payment and installment deadline?

Payment is due by December 15, 2026. If the tax due (including special rural development tax) exceeds KRW 3 million, taxpayers can apply via Hometax by December 15 to split payment interest-free through June 15, 2027. [2]

### Who can defer the 2026 Comprehensive Real Estate Holding Tax and by when?

Eligible one-household one-home owners aged 60 or older or holding a home for five years or more can apply by December 12, 2026, with tax collateral to defer payment until sale, gift, or inheritance. [2]

### What can a taxpayer do if the NTS notice differs from actual facts?

Taxpayers may file a voluntary return via Hometax by December 15, 2026, reflecting actual facts or missed exclusions, which automatically cancels the original government notice. [3][5]
