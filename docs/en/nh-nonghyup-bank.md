# NH NongHyup Bank

## NH NongHyup Bank origin and relationship with NACF

NH NongHyup Bank was created under the 2011 revision of the Agricultural Cooperatives Act, which separated the credit (financial) business and economic (agricultural marketing, distribution, and processing) business of the National Agricultural Cooperative Federation (NACF, 농협중앙회).[7] Under that restructuring effective March 2, 2012, NACF transitioned to a one-federation, two-holding-company structure: economic businesses were grouped under NongHyup Agribusiness Group (농협경제지주), while credit and insurance businesses were spun off into NongHyup Bank, NongHyup Life Insurance, and NongHyup Property & Casualty Insurance under NongHyup Financial Group (농협금융지주).[7]

Under this division of roles, NACF focuses on member cooperative and farmer education and guidance, while NH NongHyup Bank conducts commercial banking operations under the financial holding company.[7]

## NH NongHyup Bank November 2026 unstructured data quality certification

On November 27, 2026, Yonhap News, Newsis, Opinion News, and Financial Post reported, citing NH NongHyup Bank, that the bank had received the highest grade, 'Complex-type Class A', in unstructured data quality certification on November 20, 2026, from a certification institution designated by South Korea's Ministry of Science and ICT (MSIT).[2][4][5][6] South Korea's national data quality certification program operates under the Framework Act on Promotion of Data Industry and Utilization of Data to evaluate both data content quality and overall data management systems.[1][4]

According to those media reports citing the bank, NH NongHyup Bank had previously obtained 'Complex-type Class A' for structured data quality and 'Level 4' for data management systems in 2025, and expanded that recognition to unstructured data such as voice and text in 2026.[4][5] The bank stated that building 'AI-Ready Data' supports planned applications including personalized wealth-management AI, AI contact centers (AICC), internal workflow automation agents, and data distribution via the Financial Data Exchange.[2][4][5] As of November 27, 2026, the underlying certificate document, the bank's direct original press release, and the exact scope of evaluated datasets have not been independently verified from primary bank disclosures.[2][4]

## NH NongHyup Bank open innovation and alternative credit initiatives

In November 2026, NH NongHyup Bank also highlighted external AI collaborations and inclusive finance projects.[3][5] Electronic Times (ETNews) reported on November 27, 2026, that legal-AI firm CGInside won the top collaboration award (GROW category, 10 million KRW prize) at 'NH OPEN BUSINESS DAY 2026' held on November 18 for a financial-product advertising Compliance AI Agent piloted jointly with NH NongHyup Bank's Compliance Department.[3] According to ETNews, the pilot incorporated roughly 266 internal review criteria, 21 financial-product advertising templates, and 29 statutes, supervisory rules, and association guidelines.[3]

Separately, Newsis reported that NH NongHyup Bank held the 'NH Credit ON' results conference at its headquarters annex in Jung-gu, Seoul, on November 26, 2026, presenting private-sector collaborative research on alternative credit scoring for inclusive finance and plans for generative AI reporting agents.[5]
