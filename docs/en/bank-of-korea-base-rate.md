# Bank of Korea Base Rate

> The Bank of Korea Base Rate is South Korea's central bank policy rate set by the Monetary Policy Board, which raised it from 3.00% to 3.25% (+0.25 percentage points) on 2026-11-26 amid above-target 2.8% inflation and strong semiconductor-led growth.

The Bank of Korea Base Rate is the benchmark policy interest rate set by the Monetary Policy Board of the Bank of Korea to guide short-term market interest rates and achieve its 2.0 percent inflation target. On 2026-11-26, the Monetary Policy Board raised the Base Rate by 0.25 percentage points from 3.00 percent to 3.25 percent, its third hike of 2026 and the highest level since November 2024.

## Bank of Korea Base Rate role and monetary policy framework

The Bank of Korea Base Rate is the policy interest rate determined by the Bank of Korea's Monetary Policy Board as the anchor of South Korea's monetary policy framework.[1][3] Through open market operations, lending and deposit facilities, and reserve requirements, the Bank of Korea operates around the Base Rate to steer financial conditions toward its 2.0 percent inflation target.[3][6]

Changes in the Base Rate signal shifts in the Bank of Korea's monetary policy stance, though the exact timing and magnitude of pass-through to individual commercial bank deposit and loan products depend on each financial institution and are not fixed in the Monetary Policy Board's rate decision.[4][5]

## Bank of Korea Base Rate November 26, 2026 increase to 3.25%

The Bank of Korea Base Rate was raised by 0.25 percentage points from 3.00 percent to 3.25 percent on November 26, 2026, at the Monetary Policy Board's final policy meeting of 2026 chaired by Governor Hyun Song Shin.[1][4] Following consecutive 0.25 percentage point hikes in July and August and a hold in October, the November move was the third increase of 2026 and brought the Base Rate to its highest level since November 2024.[4][5]

MBC News reported that two of the seven Monetary Policy Board members cast dissenting votes in favor of holding the Base Rate at 3.00 percent.[7]

## Bank of Korea Base Rate economic backdrop and 2027 forecast

The Bank of Korea Base Rate hike on November 26, 2026, reflected persistent inflation above target alongside strong economic growth driven by semiconductor exports.[5][6] Headline consumer price inflation stood at 2.8 percent year over year in October 2026 (following 3.1 percent in August and 2.9 percent in September), and core inflation excluding food and energy also registered 2.8 percent, both exceeding the Bank of Korea's 2.0 percent inflation target.[5][6]

Meanwhile, South Korea's third-quarter 2026 real GDP grew 1.0 percent quarter over quarter on strong semiconductor exports, and the Bank of Korea sharply revised its 2027 GDP growth forecast upward from 2.9 percent to 4.1 percent.[4][5][7] Decisions at future Monetary Policy Board meetings remain unannounced as of November 26, 2026.[4]

## Bank of Korea Base Rate transmission and future meetings

The Bank of Korea Base Rate serves as the reference rate for South Korea's monetary policy operations, influencing short-term funding costs and broader deposit and lending rates across commercial banks.[3][4] Because individual banks set retail deposit and loan schedules independently, the exact timing of pass-through to household and corporate products and the outcome of future Monetary Policy Board meetings remain unverified as of November 26, 2026.[4][5]
