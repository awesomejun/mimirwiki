# National Rare Disease Designation System (South Korea)

> South Korea's National Rare Disease Designation System is an annual Korea Disease Control and Prevention Agency program that designates rare diseases to link patients to health insurance copayment relief, medical expense aid, and genomic diagnostic support. On November 24, 2026, KDCA newly designated 77 diseases, expanding the total to 1,465 pending final insurance committee approval.

The National Rare Disease Designation System (South Korea) is a statutory public health program operated by the Korea Disease Control and Prevention Agency (KDCA) under the Rare Disease Management Act to designate rare diseases for systematic management and connect patients to national medical expense and diagnostic support. Expanded annually since 2018 through applications submitted via the KDCA Rare Disease Helpline, the list is evaluated by the Rare Disease Expert Committee and the Rare Disease Management Committee. On November 24, 2026, KDCA announced the designation of 77 additional rare diseases, including multiple system atrophy and ectopia cordis, increasing the total designated count from 1,389 in 2025 to 1,465 in 2026. Final eligibility for National Health Insurance special copayment reduction and medical expense support for newly designated diseases is determined by subsequent review by the Ministry of Health and Welfare's Health Insurance Policy Deliberation Committee.

## National Rare Disease Designation System legal basis and review process

The National Rare Disease Designation System (South Korea) has been expanded annually since 2018 by the Korea Disease Control and Prevention Agency (KDCA) in accordance with the Rare Disease Management Act and its subordinate regulations.[4][6] Requests for new disease designations are accepted through the KDCA Rare Disease Helpline portal (`https://helpline.kdca.go.kr`).[2][4]

Each annual review conducts baseline disease investigations alongside consultations with medical societies and specialists before submitting candidates to the Rare Disease Expert Committee and the Rare Disease Management Committee for deliberation.[4][6] In the 2026 review cycle, KDCA evaluated 176 candidate diseases before finalizing the new additions.[4][6]

## National Rare Disease Designation System November 2026 additions

On November 24, 2026, KDCA announced the designation of 77 additional rare diseases, expanding the total number of nationally managed rare diseases from 1,389 in 2025 to 1,465 in 2026.[3][4][5] Newly designated conditions cited in the announcement include multiple system atrophy, septo-optic dysplasia, ectopia cordis, metaphyseal dysplasia, chronic thromboembolic pulmonary hypertension, and Usher syndrome.[4][6]

Although 77 diseases were newly designated, the total count increased by 76 compared with 2025 because one newly designated condition and one disease previously designated in 2021 were consolidated under a parent disease category.[4][6] Over time, the designated list has expanded from 1,014 diseases in 2019 to 1,165 in 2022, 1,314 in 2024, 1,389 in 2025, and 1,465 in 2026.[6]

## National Rare Disease Designation System linked financial and diagnostic programs

Designation under the National Rare Disease Designation System links eligible patients to public financial and diagnostic support programs.[4][6] Under the National Health Insurance Service (NHIS) Special Calculation System, designated rare diseases qualify for a reduced patient out-of-pocket copayment rate of 10% of total covered medical costs, which is scheduled to be lowered further to 7% starting in December 2026.[3][4]

In addition, patients in households earning below 140% of South Korea's standard median income may receive copayment assistance through the KDCA Rare Disease Medical Expense Support Program.[4] Newly designated diseases are also slated for inclusion in the KDCA Rare Disease Diagnostic Support Program, which supports early diagnosis through whole genome sequencing (WGS) testing for suspected rare disease patients who face diagnostic difficulty.[4]

## National Rare Disease Designation System approval timeline for newly added diseases

For patients with the 77 rare diseases newly designated in November 2026, financial assistance does not begin automatically upon KDCA's designation announcement.[4] Application of NHIS Special Calculation copayment relief and medical expense support to the newly designated diseases is to be determined through deliberation and resolution by the Ministry of Health and Welfare's Health Insurance Policy Deliberation Committee, scheduled for late November 2026.[4]

As of November 26, 2026, the committee's final deliberation outcome and the exact effective start dates for individual newly designated diseases have not yet been published.[4]

## Frequently asked questions

### What is South Korea's National Rare Disease Designation System?

It is a statutory program run by the Korea Disease Control and Prevention Agency (KDCA) under the Rare Disease Management Act that designates rare diseases each year to support systematic management, health insurance copayment reduction, medical expense aid, and genomic diagnostic testing. [4][6]

### How many rare diseases were newly designated in November 2026?

On November 24, 2026, KDCA designated 77 additional rare diseases out of 176 reviewed, bringing the total from 1,389 in 2025 to 1,465 in 2026 (a net increase of 76 after one parent-category consolidation). [4][6]

### When does financial support apply to patients with newly designated rare diseases?
