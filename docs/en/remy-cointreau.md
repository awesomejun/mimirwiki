Rémy Cointreau is a French spirits company headquartered in Paris that produces and distributes cognac, liqueurs, and other spirits across global markets. ## Rémy Cointreau business lines and brand portfolio

Rémy Cointreau operates from its headquarters at 21, Rue Balzac in Paris, France, focusing on two primary spirits segments: Cognac and Liqueurs & Spirits.[2] The Cognac portfolio consists of Rémy Martin and LOUIS XIII, while the Liqueurs & Spirits segment includes Cointreau, Metaxa, Mount Gay rum, St-Rémy, Bruichladdich, The Botanist gin, Westland Distillery, Domaine des Hautes Glaces, Belle de Brillet, and Champagne Telmont, alongside Rémy Cointreau Gastronomie.[2]

## Rémy Cointreau H1 2026-27 financial results

On November 25, 2026, Rémy Cointreau published its financial results for the first half of fiscal year 2026-27, reporting group sales of 510.9 million euros, up 4.4% as reported and up 2.9% on an organic basis.[1][3][5] Gross margin stood at 67.7% (-0.3 percentage points as reported, -1.5 percentage points organic), while advertising and promotion (A&P) expenditure represented 16.3% of sales, with investments reduced in the first half and scheduled to increase in the second half.[1]

Current operating profit (COP) returned to growth at 126.7 million euros, rising 16.6% as reported and 7.8% organically, lifting the current operating margin to 24.8% (+2.6 percentage points as reported, +1.1 percentage points organic).[1][5] Net profit declined 9.8% as reported (-21.2% organic) to 57 million euros.[5] Recurring free cash flow turned positive at 42.0 million euros compared with -12.6 million euros in H1 2025-26, and the leverage ratio improved to 2.87x from 3.22x at March 31, 2026.[1]

## Rémy Cointreau reported versus organic growth metrics

Rémy Cointreau reports both reported (as-published) and organic growth rates in its financial disclosures.[1][5] Reported growth includes the impact of foreign currency exchange rate movements and scope changes during the period, whereas organic growth isolates underlying sales and profit performance at constant exchange rates and comparable scope.[1][5]

In H1 2026-27, this distinction produced a 1.5 percentage point gap in group sales growth (+4.4% reported versus +2.9% organic) and an 8.8 percentage point gap in current operating profit growth (+16.6% reported versus +7.8% organic).[1][5] Global travel retail sales rose 47.3% year on year against a low prior-year comparison base, and Rémy Cointreau highlighted travel retail, emerging markets, and expanding its footprint in China among its key commercial priorities.[5]

## Rémy Cointreau 2026-27 guidance and 2031-32 objectives

Following its first-half performance, Rémy Cointreau raised parts of its full-year 2026-27 guidance on November 25, 2026.[1] The group maintained its expectation for a return to organic sales growth, upgraded its current operating margin outlook from a slight organic improvement to a solid organic improvement, introduced a recurring free cash flow conversion target of around 20%, and lowered its March 31, 2027 leverage ratio ceiling (excluding hybrid bond impact) from below 3.5x to below 3.2x.[1]

Alongside the interim results, Rémy Cointreau unveiled strategic priorities and medium-term objectives extending to 2031-32, including a plan to double travel retail sales.[1][5][6] As of November 26, 2026, whether Rémy Cointreau achieves its full-year 2026-27 guidance and 2031-32 medium-term targets remains to be determined.[1][6]
