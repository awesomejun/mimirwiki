# Cloudflare

> Cloudflare, Inc. is a San Francisco-based technology company listed on the New York Stock Exchange as NET. It runs a global network that provides CDN, DNS, DDoS protection, web application firewall, Zero Trust and serverless developer services such as Workers and R2. Its Form 10-K reports fiscal year 2025 revenue of $2,167.9 million and 5,156 full-time employees.

Cloudflare, Inc. is a United States technology company, incorporated in Delaware in July 2009 and headquartered in San Francisco, California, that operates a global network delivering application security and performance services, a SASE platform and a developer platform that includes Cloudflare Workers and R2 object storage. Its Class A common stock has been listed on the New York Stock Exchange under the symbol NET since September 13, 2019. For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, Cloudflare reported revenue of $2,167.9 million and 5,156 full-time employees. Cloudflare lists Free, Pro and Business plans with public prices alongside a custom-priced Contract tier.

## Cloudflare corporate record and SEC filings

Cloudflare, Inc. is registered with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission under Central Index Key (CIK) 0001477333, with Delaware as its state of incorporation, a fiscal year ending December 31 and Standard Industrial Classification code 7372 (prepackaged software).[1] Cloudflare was incorporated in Delaware in July 2009, and its principal executive offices are at 101 Townsend Street, San Francisco, California 94107.[2] Cloudflare's official company history gives 2009 as the founding year, names Matthew Prince, Michelle Zatlyn and Lee Holloway as founders, and dates the public launch of the service to September 27, 2010, at the TechCrunch Disrupt conference in San Francisco.[10]

Cloudflare's Class A common stock has been listed on the New York Stock Exchange under the symbol NET since September 13, 2019.[2] Cloudflare has two classes of common stock: Class B shares carry 10 votes per share and Class A shares carry one vote per share.[2]

Cloudflare's most recent annual report is the Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025 (fiscal year 2025), which [SEC EDGAR](https://mimirwiki.com/wiki/sec-edgar) lists as filed on February 26, 2026.[1] It reports the following figures.[2]

## Cloudflare application services plan prices

Cloudflare publishes four application services plans on its pricing page, under the tab labeled Network & CDN: Free, Pro, Business and Contract.[5] As of 2026-11-21 the page lists the following prices in US dollars.[5]

| Plan | Listed price | Billing terms | Intended use stated by Cloudflare |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 per month | No charge | Personal or hobby projects |
| Pro | $20 per month | Billed annually; $25 per month if billed monthly | Professional websites |
| Business | $200 per month | Billed annually; $250 per month if billed monthly | Small businesses operating online |
| Contract | Custom | Billed annually | Mission-critical applications |

Cloudflare Pro and Business prices differ by billing period: the listed monthly-billing price is $5 per month higher than the annual-billing price for Pro and $50 per month higher for Business.[5] The Contract tier has no published price, and its link on the pricing page leads to Cloudflare's enterprise packages page.[5] The plan comparison on the same page marks DNS, unmetered DDoS protection, a [content delivery network](https://mimirwiki.com/wiki/content-delivery-network) (CDN), a Universal SSL certificate and a web application firewall (WAF) as included in all four plans, and lists a 100% uptime service level agreement for the Business and Contract tiers only.[5]

Cloudflare's pricing page also lists three SASE and Zero Trust plans as of 2026-11-21: Free at $0 with a limit of 50 users, Pay-as-you-go at $7 per user per month, and Contract with a custom annual price per user.[5]

## Cloudflare Workers plans, limits and pricing

[Cloudflare Workers](https://mimirwiki.com/wiki/cloudflare-workers) is the serverless compute product of Cloudflare's developer platform, offered on a Workers Free plan and on a Workers Paid plan that has a minimum charge of $5 per account per month.[6] Workers Paid usage follows the Standard usage model, which bills by number of requests and by CPU time, with no charge for duration and no additional charge for data transfer (egress) or bandwidth.[6] The figures below were read from Cloudflare's documentation on 2026-11-21; the pricing rows come from the Workers pricing page and the limit rows from the Workers limits page.[6][7]

| Item | Workers Free | Workers Paid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Requests | 100,000 per day | 10 million per month included, then $0.30 per additional million |
| CPU time | 10 ms per invocation | 30 million CPU ms per month included, then $0.02 per additional million CPU ms |
| Maximum CPU time per HTTP request | 10 ms | 5 minutes (default 30 seconds) |
| Memory per isolate | 128 MB | 128 MB |
| Subrequests per request | 50 | 10,000 |
| Worker size | 64 MiB | 64 MiB |
| Workers per account | 100 | 500 |
| Cron Triggers per account | 5 | 1,000 |

The Cloudflare Workers Free daily request limit resets at midnight UTC, and Cloudflare returns Error 1027 when a Worker exceeds it.[7] Cloudflare does not bill for subrequests that a Worker makes.[6]

Cloudflare's developer documentation is also published in forms intended for automated readers: as of 2026-11-21, the Workers documentation pages link to an [llms.txt](https://mimirwiki.com/wiki/llms-txt) index, an `llms-full.txt` file and a Markdown alternate of each page, and the documentation navigation links to Cloudflare's [Model Context Protocol](https://mimirwiki.com/wiki/model-context-protocol) servers on GitHub.[6]

## Cloudflare R2, D1, Workers KV, Queues and Durable Objects pricing

[Cloudflare R2](https://mimirwiki.com/wiki/cloudflare-r2) is Cloudflare's object storage service, and its pricing page states that there are no charges for egress bandwidth for any storage class.[8] R2 bills for the volume of data stored and for two classes of operations, and a data retrieval fee applies only to the Infrequent Access storage class.[8] The prices below were read on 2026-11-21.[8]

| R2 item | Standard storage | Infrequent Access storage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Storage | $0.015 per GB-month | $0.01 per GB-month |
| Class A operations | $4.50 per million requests | $9.00 per million requests |
| Class B operations | $0.36 per million requests | $0.90 per million requests |
| Data retrieval | None | $0.01 per GB |
| Egress to the Internet | Free | Free |

The Cloudflare R2 free tier covers 10 GB-month of storage, 1 million Class A requests and 10 million Class B requests per month, and applies only to Standard storage.[8] Cloudflare notes that other metered services connected to an R2 bucket may charge separately.[8]

Cloudflare prices its other developer platform data products on the Workers pricing page.[6] The following allowances and rates were listed on 2026-11-21.[6]

| Product and metric | Workers Free | Workers Paid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D1 rows read | 5 million per day | 25 billion per month included, then $0.001 per million rows |
| D1 rows written | 100,000 per day | 50 million per month included, then $1.00 per million rows |
| D1 storage | 5 GB in total | 5 GB included, then $0.75 per GB-month |
| Workers KV keys read | 100,000 per day | 10 million per month included, then $0.50 per million |
| Workers KV keys written | 1,000 per day | 1 million per month included, then $5.00 per million |
| Workers KV stored data | 1 GB | 1 GB included, then $0.50 per GB-month |
| Queues standard operations | 10,000 per day | 1 million per month included, then $0.40 per million |
| Durable Objects requests | 100,000 per day | 1 million per month included, then $0.15 per million |
| Durable Objects duration | 13,000 GB-s per day | 400,000 GB-s per month included, then $12.50 per million GB-s |

On the Cloudflare Workers Free plan, only Durable Objects that use the SQLite storage backend are available.[6] Cloudflare states that D1 and Queues carry no data transfer (egress) or bandwidth charges.[6]

## Cloudflare AI crawler controls and Web Bot Auth

Cloudflare has announced a series of controls that let site owners decide how AI crawlers reach content served through its network.[11][14] On July 1, 2025, Cloudflare announced that every new domain would be asked at sign-up whether to allow AI crawlers, and described the change as blocking, by default, AI crawlers that access content without permission or compensation.[11]

On the same date Cloudflare announced pay per crawl in private beta.[12] Under pay per crawl a site owner sets a flat per-request price for the whole domain, and a crawler either signals payment intent in request headers or receives an HTTP 402 Payment Required response; Cloudflare acts as the merchant of record.[12]

[Web Bot Auth](https://mimirwiki.com/wiki/web-bot-auth) is the bot authentication approach, based on HTTP Message Signatures, that Cloudflare uses to verify the identity of crawlers and agents.[13] Cloudflare published a proposal to identify bots and agents with HTTP Message Signatures, together with an IETF draft for Web Bot Auth, in May 2025, and announced on July 1, 2025 that it was integrating HTTP Message Signatures into its Verified Bots Program.[13] A crawler that takes part in pay per crawl generates an Ed25519 key pair, publishes the public key in a hosted directory and signs each request with HTTP Message Signatures.[12]

On July 1, 2026, Cloudflare introduced controls, available to all customers including the Free tier, that manage AI traffic by three behaviors: Search, Agent and Training.[14] In the same announcement Cloudflare said that from September 15, 2026 new domains would by default block Training and Agent traffic on pages that display ads, while Search would remain allowed.[14]

On September 15, 2026, Cloudflare announced a Disallow AI Training setting, which publishes a no-training preference in robots.txt, keeps mixed-use crawlers that Cloudflare designates as Accountable allowed for search, and blocks other training crawlers.[15] The announcement names Apple, Google and Microsoft as operators that honor or have committed to honor the setting, and states that the earlier Block AI Bots option would be deprecated in favor of the Search, Training and Agent controls.[15]

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does Cloudflare Pro cost?

As of 2026-11-21, Cloudflare's pricing page lists the Pro plan at $20 per month when billed annually, or $25 per month when billed monthly. The Business plan is listed at $200 per month billed annually, or $250 per month billed monthly. The Free plan is $0, and the top tier, listed as Contract, has custom pricing billed annually. [5]

### Does Cloudflare R2 charge egress fees?

No. As of 2026-11-21, the Cloudflare R2 pricing page states that there are no charges for egress bandwidth for any storage class. R2 charges for storage ($0.015 per GB-month for Standard storage) and for Class A and Class B operations, and other metered services connected to a bucket may charge separately. [8]

### What are the limits of the Cloudflare Workers free plan?

As of 2026-11-21, the Workers Free plan allows 100,000 requests per day, 10 milliseconds of CPU time per invocation, 128 MB of memory and 50 subrequests per request. The Workers Paid plan has a minimum charge of $5 per month and includes 10 million requests and 30 million CPU milliseconds per month. [6][7]

### Does Cloudflare block AI crawlers by default?

On July 1, 2025, Cloudflare announced that every new domain would be asked at sign-up whether to allow AI crawlers. On July 1, 2026 it added separate Search, Agent and Training controls for all plans and announced that from September 15, 2026 new domains would by default block Training and Agent traffic on pages that display ads, while allowing Search. Site owners can change these settings. [11][14]

### Is Cloudflare SOC 2 and ISO 27001 certified?

As of 2026-11-21, Cloudflare's compliance resources page lists SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001:2022, ISO 27701:2019, PCI DSS 4.0 and FedRAMP High, among other certifications and attestations. Cloudflare states that the supporting compliance documentation is available through its dashboard. [9]

### What is Cloudflare's stock ticker and where is it listed?

Cloudflare, Inc. Class A common stock has been listed on the New York Stock Exchange under the symbol NET since September 13, 2019. The company files with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission under CIK 0001477333. [1][2]
