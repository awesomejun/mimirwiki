# Seasonal Influenza Surveillance in South Korea

> Seasonal influenza surveillance in South Korea is a sentinel monitoring system run by the Korea Disease Control and Prevention Agency (KDCA) tracking outpatient influenza-like illness (ILI) rates, laboratory virus detection rates, and sentinel hospitalizations. For Week 47 (November 15-21, 2026), KDCA reported 41.1 ILI cases per 1,000 outpatients (fifth straight weekly rise) and a 37.7% virus detection rate, up from 33.1%.

Seasonal influenza surveillance in South Korea is a nationwide sentinel monitoring system operated by the Korea Disease Control and Prevention Agency (KDCA) that tracks seasonal flu activity using clinical symptom reports and respiratory laboratory specimens from designated clinics and hospitals. For Week 47 of 2026 (November 15-21), KDCA reported that the outpatient influenza-like illness (ILI) rate rose for the fifth consecutive week to 41.1 per 1,000 outpatients (up from 37.0 in Week 46), while the influenza virus detection rate among clinic respiratory specimens increased from 33.1% to 37.7%, with sentinel hospital admissions reaching 970.

## What the ILI rate and specimen detection rate each measure

KDCA publishes weekly Infectious Disease Sentinel Surveillance Bulletins that separate clinical symptom surveillance from laboratory pathogen surveillance.[1][2][3] The influenza-like illness (ILI) rate (`의사환자 분율`) counts the number of outpatients presenting with suspected influenza symptoms per 1,000 total outpatients visiting designated sentinel clinics; it measures clinical suspicion rather than laboratory-confirmed cases.[5]

By contrast, the specimen detection rate (`검출률`) measures the percentage of respiratory samples collected from suspected respiratory-infection outpatients at sentinel clinics that test laboratory-positive for influenza virus, identifying circulating subtypes such as A(H1N1)pdm09.[5] Hospital-level and tertiary-hospital admission counts reflect participating sentinel institutions rather than total nationwide hospitalizations.[5]

## Week 47 (November 15-21, 2026) surveillance findings

Following an early autumn epidemic advisory issued on September 11, 2026, South Korea's outpatient ILI rate declined to 17.7 in Week 42 before rising for five consecutive weeks through Week 47 (19.2 in Week 43, 22.8 in Week 44, 28.9 in Week 45, 37.0 in Week 46, and 41.1 in Week 47), crossing 40 for the first time in the 2026-2027 season (though lower than 70.9 in the same week a year earlier).[4][5]

## Hospitalization burden and national immunization coverage

Hospital-level sentinel admissions rose for four consecutive weeks to 970 in Week 47, about 1.6 times the 605 recorded in the same week a year earlier and already above the previous season's Week 48 peak of 708.[4] Adults aged 65 and older accounted for 53% of hospitalized influenza patients.[4]

Under South Korea's national immunization program for seniors aged 65 and older, pregnant individuals, and children aged 6 months to 14 years, vaccination coverage as of November 22, 2026, stood at 75.2% for seniors (-3.3 percentage points year-on-year) and 62.9% for children (-0.7 percentage points).[4] KDCA emphasized vaccination and noted that influenza and COVID-19 vaccines can be co-administered at designated clinics and public health centers.[4]

## Unverified seasonal trajectory as of November 2026

As of November 27, 2026, the Week 48 trend and the eventual timing and height of the 2026-2027 seasonal influenza epidemic peak in South Korea remain unverified.[4][5]

## Frequently asked questions

### What is seasonal influenza surveillance in South Korea?

It is a weekly sentinel surveillance system operated by the Korea Disease Control and Prevention Agency (KDCA) that monitors influenza-like illness among clinic outpatients, laboratory virus detection in respiratory specimens, and hospitalizations at participating hospitals. [1][5]

### What do the ILI rate and specimen positivity rate each count?

The ILI rate counts outpatients with suspected flu symptoms per 1,000 total outpatients at sentinel clinics (not confirmed cases). The specimen detection rate counts the share of respiratory samples from suspected respiratory-infection outpatients that test laboratory-positive for influenza virus. [5]

### What were the Week 47 (November 15-21, 2026) numbers?

KDCA reported 41.1 ILI cases per 1,000 outpatients (up from 37.0), a 37.7% virus detection rate (up from 33.1%), 970 hospital-level sentinel admissions, and 135 tertiary-hospital sentinel admissions. [4][5]

### Do sentinel hospital admission numbers represent every hospitalized patient in South Korea?

No. KDCA's hospital-level (970) and tertiary-hospital (135) figures count admissions reported by participating sentinel hospitals to track trends rather than total nationwide hospitalizations. [5]
