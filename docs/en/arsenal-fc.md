## Arsenal FC 1-1 draw with Borussia Dortmund (2026-11-24)

Arsenal FC drew 1-1 with Borussia Dortmund at Emirates Stadium on Tuesday, 2026-11-24 in the UEFA Champions League league phase, remaining unbeaten in the league phase.[1][4] Viktor Gyokeres opened the scoring in the 10th minute with a long-range strike that Arsenal reported as the club's 400th goal in the competition.[1][5]

Borussia Dortmund equalized in the second half through a left-footed finish by Maximilian Beier, and a subsequent effort by Jobe Bellingham was ruled out for offside.[1][5] Goal.com reported that Arsenal took 30 shots across the match (four on target) and hit the woodwork three times, while Arsenal's match summary noted hitting the post twice in search of a winner.[1][5] Following the draw, manager Mikel Arteta described his side's inability to finish off Dortmund as "unbelievable."[5]
